Cage Lights and Alarm Strobes

The 16 Cage Lights and the green, amber and red Alarm Strobes - mounting, the settings screen, light modes, the six siren tones, and switching them from redstone or ELCL.

Cage Lights and Alarm Strobes are signal devices: lamps and beacons for a control room, a warehouse floor or an alarm system. They work from plain redstone on their own, and on a network ELCL can switch them too. The Speakers are the third kind of signal device and share everything in What they share.

What they share#

  • Mounting. They go on any face of a block (floor, ceiling or wall), pointing away from it.
  • On their own they work from the redstone at their block.
  • On a network. Cabled to a network, or placed against a networked block, each is also a device there: one lane, signals.signalDeviceDrain (default 0.25) FE/t, named LGT01 (lights), SRN01 (strobes) or SPK01 (speakers), and listed in Work with Devices.
  • The settings screen. Right-click one to open it (on a network, with the Firewall's BUILD permission). Click a row to step its value, right-click to step back, hold Shift for steps of one. Text rows (the device name, a Speaker's URL or notes) are edited in the field at the bottom: Enter sets it, Esc leaves it.
  • The popup. Look at one for its state, mode and device name.
  • ELCL. Their commands take DEV() as a list of up to 32 devices, or *ALL for every one of that kind on the network, and need the Firewall's BUILD permission.

Cage Lights#

A Cage Light is a caged lamp, one for each of the 16 dye colours:

  • Black Cage Light
  • Blue Cage Light
  • Brown Cage Light
  • Cyan Cage Light
  • Gray Cage Light
  • Green Cage Light
  • Light Blue Cage Light
  • Light Gray Cage Light
  • Lime Cage Light
  • Magenta Cage Light
  • Orange Cage Light
  • Pink Cage Light
  • Purple Cage Light
  • Red Cage Light
  • White Cage Light
  • Yellow Cage Light

Four come from iron bars, glass, glowstone dust, iron nuggets and the dye:

SettingValues
ControlRedstone (the default: on with a signal), Inverted (on without one), Always on, or Network only
LevelThe light level it gives while lit, 1-15 (default 15)

The network's state, set by CHGLGT, adds to the redstone's: either one turns the light on. Always on ignores both; Network only ignores the redstone.

CHGLGT DEV(LGT01 LGT02) STATUS(*ON) LVL(15)
CHGLGT DEV(*ALL) STATUS(*TOGGLE)

A level outside 1-15 is ELC2401.

Alarm Strobes#

An Alarm Strobe is a flashing beacon with a sounder, in three colours whose lenses match the lime, orange and red Cage Lights: Green Alarm Strobe, Amber Alarm Strobe and Red Alarm Strobe. Each is made from redstone, a note block, an iron ingot, glass and the dye:

SettingValues
Light modeSolid, Slow flash, Medium flash or Fast flash (the default)
SoundWail (the default), Yelp, Klaxon, Bell, Horn, Beep, or Silent (light only)
Volume0-100 % (default 80)
RangeHow far the sound carries, in blocks (default 48, at most signals.sirenMaxRange (default 96))
TriggerRedstone, Network (ELCL only) or Both (the default)

While its trigger says so, the strobe is active: the lens lights in its light mode and the tone plays, looping. The tone starts again at the end of each sample, and strobes on one network sounding the same tone count their samples from the same moment, so a whole hall of them wails together.

ToneSample length
Wail80 ticks
Yelp40 ticks
Klaxon32 ticks
Bell40 ticks
Horn40 ticks
Beep24 ticks

STRSRN starts strobes from the network (optionally changing their sound and mode) and ENDSRN stops them. A strobe whose trigger is Redstone takes no network commands (ELC2408).

STRSRN DEV(*ALL) SOUND(*KLAXON) MODE(*FAST)   /* every strobe on the network */
DLYJOB DLY(30)
ENDSRN DEV(*ALL)

The sounds' subtitles are listed on Sounds. Programs that raise alarms from storage levels, power or device faults are on Redstone, PLCs and signals from ELCL and Worked examples.
