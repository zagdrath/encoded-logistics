Terminals and access

The ways into a network's storage - terminals on cables, consoles in racks, a terminal you carry, and the Terminal Desk.

A network's storage is only useful if you can get at it. Encoded Logistics gives you four ways in, and they all show the same thing: every item, fluid and gas the network holds (on hot storage and on tape), what it can craft, and the energy on its Energy Storage Drives.

Way inWhat it isWhere it worksPage
Access Terminal and Fabrication TerminalCable parts with a storage grid (the Fabrication Terminal adds a 3x3 crafting grid)Where you mount themAccess and Fabrication Terminals
Rack Console, Rack Fabrication Console, Rack Schematic ConsoleThe same terminals in a 1U rack drawerAt a Server RackRack Consoles
Handheld TerminalAn Access Terminal you carry, with its own batteryIn range of a Relay Antenna, or anywhere through a Wireless ControllerHandheld Terminal
Terminal DeskA green-screen terminal running the Terminal OSAt the deskTerminal Desk and Swivel Chair

The first three use the same terminal screen: a search field, a sortable grid, tabs for items, fluids, gases and energy, and crafting requests. Learn it once on the Access and Fabrication Terminals page and it works the same everywhere. The Terminal Desk is different: it runs the Terminal OS, a command-driven system you can program in ELCL (see Terminal OS and ELCL).

Reading order#

  1. Access and Fabrication Terminals: the terminal screen itself, and the Access and Fabrication Terminals.
  2. Rack Consoles: the rack versions, once you have a Server Rack.
  3. Handheld Terminal: taking the terminal with you.
  4. Terminal Desk and Swivel Chair: the desk, its drawer and the Swivel Chair.

Who may use a terminal#

On a network with a Firewall, opening a terminal needs the View permission, putting items in needs Insert, taking them out Extract, and asking for a craft Craft. A network without a Firewall lets everyone do everything. The Schematic Terminal, which lists the network's schematics and crafting jobs, is covered with crafting on the jobs page.
