Handheld Terminal

An Access Terminal you carry - linking it to a network, how far it reaches through Relay Antennas or a Wireless Controller, its battery and its signal bars.

On this page
  1. Linking it
  2. Opening it
  3. Range and signal
  4. Battery
  5. Permissions

The Handheld Terminal is an Access Terminal in your pocket. It opens the same storage screen, from anywhere its network can reach you. It needs three things to open: a link to a network, reach to that network (a Relay Antenna covering you, or a Wireless Controller), and charge in its battery.

Linking it#

There are two ways to link a Handheld Terminal, and they reach differently.

Link it by using it onIt reaches youNeeds
A Relay Antenna or a Network Controller blockInside the coverage of any of that network's Relay Antennas, in the same dimensionRelay Antennas where you want to use it
A Wireless Controller in a rack (its unit through the open front door, or anywhere on its rack)Anywhere in the dimension, and in other dimensions while rack.wirelessCrossDimension (default true)A Wireless Controller online on the network
  • Relay Antenna or Network Controller: the action bar says "Linked to network #n". Linking this way also clears any earlier Wireless Controller link.
  • Wireless Controller: the action bar says "Linked to this Wireless Controller (network n): works anywhere". This needs Rack access on the rack's network, as anything you do at a rack does. The controller remembers you (its panel's Linked tab lists the players it serves, and an X there unlinks one). A terminal linked to a controller still uses the network's Relay Antennas too, when one covers you.

The terminal must be linked to a network that is running. Used on a block that isn't on one, it says "Not on a working network".

While rack.wirelessAnyController (default true), any online Wireless Controller on the network serves a terminal linked through one of them, so a second controller backs up the first. With it off, only the controller it was linked to will do. More on Wireless Controllers on the Wireless page.

You don't need Access Points for Handheld Terminals: Access Points are for wireless clients (Wireless Bridges and Ports), not for terminals.

Opening it#

Use it in the air (right-click with it in your hand, main hand or off hand). If it isn't linked, the action bar says "Not linked"; with an empty battery "Battery empty"; and with no antenna or controller reaching you "Out of range".

The screen is the terminal screen described on Access and Fabrication Terminals, with a side panel showing:

  • the battery bar,
  • a link light,
  • signal bars, 0 to 4.

If you walk out of range while it's open, the grid says why it can't reach the network.

Range and signal#

Through Relay Antennas. A Relay Antenna covers a sphere around itself of reach.relayBaseRange (default 32) blocks, plus reach.relayRangePerTransceiver (default 32) blocks for each Optical Transceiver in its four slots. The terminal uses whichever of its network's antennas covers you best (the nearest relative to its range), picked again every half second as you move. Antenna coverage works only in the antenna's own dimension.

The signal bars show how deep in an antenna's coverage you are:

Your distance, as a share of the antenna's rangeBars
Up to a quarter4
Up to a half3
Up to three quarters2
Out to the edge1
Not covered0

Through a Wireless Controller. No range limit and full bars, as long as the controller is online (and, across dimensions, rack.wirelessCrossDimension is on).

The terminal's icon shows its link state: not linked, linked (in reach), or out of range. It updates once a second while the terminal is in your inventory.

Battery#

The terminal holds reach.handheldCapacity (default 200,000) FE. It uses:

  • reach.handheldDrainPerSecond (default 10) FE a second while its screen is open, and
  • reach.handheldEnergyPerItem (default 1) FE for every item you move with it.

The tooltip shows the charge. To charge it:

  • In any FE charger from another mod: it takes up to reach.handheldChargeRate (default 4096) FE/t.
  • At a Capacitor Bank: use the terminal on the bank and hold the button. It draws up to reach.handheldChargeRate FE a tick from the bank until it's full or you look away.

Permissions#

The terminal works with your own Firewall permissions on its network, the same as a terminal on a cable: View to open it, Insert, Extract and Craft for the rest. See Firewall and security.
