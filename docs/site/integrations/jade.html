Jade

The Jade tooltips Encoded Logistics adds, and the blocks it keeps out of Jade because they have popups of their own.

With Jade installed, looking at some of the mod's blocks adds a few lines to Jade's tooltip. Jade's own energy bar reads any block's FE, so it also shows, for example, a Network Controller structure's whole buffer.

BlockJade shows
Network ControllerWhether the network is online, its lanes in use against its capacity ("0 / 32 lanes"), and the structure ("3x3x3 frame, 20 blocks")
Network, Dense and Fiber CablesThe lanes running through it ("Lanes 8 / 32")
Power InletWhat it is receiving ("Receiving 512 FE/t")
Segment Isolator"Isolating two segments" or "Idle"
Lithography PressIts progress ("Exposure 40%")
Drive BayHow many drives it holds ("3 / 10 drives")

Each of these can be turned on or off in Jade's own plugin settings, under Encoded Logistics.

Names#

A cable with parts or a facade on it, and the internal block that holds parts on a block face, are named in Jade after the part or facade you are looking at, rather than "Network Cable".

Looking at any block of a Midrange machine that takes more than one block of room (a Tape Drive or an Integrated Midrange System, say) shows the machine's main block, so you see the reel in a Tape Drive even from its top.

Blocks Jade leaves alone#

These have their own popups, so Jade shows nothing for them:

  • the Server Rack (a popup for each unit you look at);
  • the Small Wireless Bridge;
  • the Midrange Disk Drive and Tape Drive.
