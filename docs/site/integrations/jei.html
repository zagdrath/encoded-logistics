JEI

What Encoded Logistics adds to JEI - the Lithography category, crafting stations, recipe transfer, dragging items onto filters and ghost slots, and search sync with the terminals.

On this page
  1. The Lithography category
  2. Crafting stations and info
  3. Recipe transfer (the "+" button)
  4. Dragging onto filters and ghost slots
  5. Search sync

With JEI installed, the mod's crafting, smelting and other vanilla-type recipes show up in JEI as any mod's do. On top of that, the mod adds the following.

The Lithography category#

Lithography Press recipes have a category of their own, laid out like the press: the photomask above, the wafer on the left, the additive below and the result on the right, with the energy and time underneath. The photomask is marked "Not consumed": it is never used up. The Lithography Press is the category's station, so looking up its uses shows every lithography recipe. The recipes are the ones the server sends, so a datapack's changes show too. See Lithography.

Crafting stations and info#

  • The Fabricator is listed as a station for crafting recipes, since it runs them for the network's crafting jobs.
  • The Network Controller has an information page on how controllers form structures and what they provide.

Recipe transfer (the "+" button)#

WhereWhat "+" does
A Fabrication Terminal (any crafting recipe)Fills the crafting grid from the network first, then your inventory. Before that, the recipe marks what is missing: red for missing, blue for missing but craftable on the network. The "+" still works then, moving what there is
A Schematic Encoder (any recipe)A crafting recipe fills the ghost grid in crafting mode; any other recipe becomes a processing schematic with its inputs (up to nine) and outputs (up to three) and their amounts. Only ghost entries are set: nothing is taken from anywhere

Dragging onto filters and ghost slots#

Drag an item from JEI's list onto a ghost slot to set it, as clicking with the item would:

  • the filters on ports, Inventory Taps, Collector and Deployer Planes, and the Threshold Sensor's item;
  • the Gateway's stock list;
  • the Schematic Encoder's grid and outputs;
  • the filter boxes in the Server Rack's screen (a Router or L3 Switch route, an L3 Switch QoS rule);
  • the Rack Elevation editor: drag a rack device onto a free unit to place it there.

Dragging a fluid (an Arcforge gas is one too) sets a fluid or gas entry.

Search sync#

The terminals can keep their search field in step with JEI's search bar, both ways. Turn it on with the client option terminal.searchSynced (default false) (Sync Search with JEI). See Access and Fabrication Terminals.
