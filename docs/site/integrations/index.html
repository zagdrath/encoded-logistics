Integrations

What Encoded Logistics adds when Arcforge, JEI or Jade is installed alongside it.

Encoded Logistics needs no other mod, but it works with three optional ones. Each integration switches itself on when the other mod is installed and does nothing otherwise; there is nothing to configure.

ModWhat you getPage
Arcforge (2.5.0 or later)Arcforge machines on the network through the Small Wireless Bridge (read, control, power and feed them), Arcforge gases in Pressurized drives, and Arc Crusher dusts for the mod's metalsArcforge
JEIThe Lithography recipe category, recipe transfer into the Fabrication Terminal and the Schematic Encoder, dragging items onto filters and ghost slots, and the terminals' search syncJEI
JadeTooltips for the Network Controller, cables, the Power Inlet, the Segment Isolator, the Lithography Press and the Drive BayJade

Developers who want to see how an integration is put together can read The Arcforge integration.
