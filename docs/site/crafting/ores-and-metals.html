Ores and metals

Neodymium, tantalum and gallium - where their ores generate, mining and smelting them, dusts with Arcforge, and the storage blocks.

On this page
  1. Where the ores are
  2. Mining
  3. Ingots
  4. Dusts (with Arcforge)
  5. Storage blocks

The mod adds three metals. Neodymium and tantalum open up the middle of the mod: 128K and larger dies and drives, Tantalum Capacitors, Pressurized and Energy drives, ports and the Gateway. Gallium is for the top of it: Processor Dies, Optical Transceivers and Radio Modules. Each has an ore, a raw metal, an ingot and a storage block. Everything before them (Logic and Memory Dies, the first network, the Midrange line) needs only vanilla materials.

Where the ores are#

All three generate in the Overworld only, in every Overworld biome.

OreHeightVeinsVein sizeNotes
Neodymium Ore, Deepslate Neodymium Orey -32 to 48, most around y 88 per chunkup to 8The commonest. Generates whether or not it's exposed to air
Tantalum Ore, Deepslate Tantalum OreThe bottom of the world (y -64) to y -8, evenly4 per chunkup to 6Below y -8 it's mostly in deepslate. 40% of veins touching air are skipped
Deepslate Gallium OreThe bottom of the world (y -64) to y -32, most around y -482 per chunkup to 4Deepslate only, the deepest and rarest. 60% of veins touching air are skipped

"Most around" is the peak of a triangular spread: the ore is commonest there and thins out towards both ends. Because tantalum and gallium avoid air, look for them by branch mining rather than in caves.

Mining#

Every ore needs an iron pickaxe or better. Deepslate ores are harder to break, as vanilla's are.

Mined normally, an ore drops one raw metal (Raw Neodymium, Raw Tantalum, Raw Gallium), more with Fortune. With Silk Touch it drops the ore block. The ores are in the common c:ores tags (c:ores/neodymium and so on), so other mods' ore processing recognises them.

Ingots#

Smelt or blast the raw metal, the ore block itself or the dust into an ingot: Neodymium Ingot, Tantalum Ingot, Gallium Ingot. The furnace and the blast furnace both work, for all three inputs.

Dusts (with Arcforge)#

Neodymium Dust, Tantalum Dust and Gallium Dust are made only by Arcforge's Arc Crusher. Without Arcforge installed there's no way to make them (the recipes simply don't load), though a dust you have still smelts into an ingot.

Arc Crusher inputGives
Ore2 dust, and a 25% chance of 1 more
Raw metal1 dust, and a 25% chance of 1 more
Ingot1 dust

Each takes 200 ticks. So with Arcforge, crushing ore before smelting gives more ingots. See Arcforge.

Storage blocks#

Nine ingots make a block, and a block crafts back into nine:

BlockFrom
Block of Neodymium9 Neodymium Ingots
Block of Tantalum9 Tantalum Ingots
Block of Gallium9 Gallium Ingots
Block of Ferrite9 Ferrite

They need an iron pickaxe to mine, and are in the c:storage_blocks tags. The three metal blocks (not Ferrite) can be used in a beacon's base.
