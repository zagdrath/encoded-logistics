Lithography

The Lithography Press, the four photomasks it keeps, and the Logic, Memory, Processor and 8K Storage Dies it etches, with the power and time each takes.

On this page
  1. Using the press
  2. Automation
  3. Photomasks
  4. The dies
  5. The recipe type

Dies are the chips at the heart of the mod's devices, and the Lithography Press is the only way to make them. It etches a pattern onto a wafer using a photomask, which it keeps: you make each photomask once and use it for every die of that kind.

The press is a machine of its own: it needs FE but no network. Encoded Logistics has no generator, so power it from another mod's generator, battery or cable. It takes FE on every face.

Using the press#

The press has four slots:

SlotTakes
PhotomaskThe photomask for the die you want. Never used up
WaferA Silicon Wafer or Doped Silicon
AdditiveRedstone, Ferrite or a Gallium Ingot, by recipe
OutputThe finished die

Put a photomask, a wafer and the additive in, and give it power. While the inputs match a recipe and the output has room, the press spends the recipe's energy evenly over its time. If it runs out of power it pauses and keeps its progress. At the end it uses up one wafer and one additive and puts the die in the output.

It buffers lithography.pressCapacity (default 32,000) FE and takes in up to lithography.pressMaxInput (default 512) FE a tick, which is enough for any recipe at full speed. The screen shows the buffer, the exposure progress and its status; the window lights while it works, and a comparator reads its progress (0 to 15).

Automation#

  • The top face takes photomasks into the photomask slot.
  • The sides take wafers and additives, each into its own slot (wafers to the wafer slot first).
  • The bottom gives out finished dies only. The photomask never comes out by automation.

So a hopper or an Ingress or Egress Port on its sides and bottom makes it a die factory. You can also run it from autocrafting with a Gateway and a Processing Schematic.

Photomasks#

Each photomask is a ring of glass and Ferrite around a centre that sets its pattern. They stack to 1.

The three die tiers set the mod's progression:

  • Logic Dies need only vanilla materials. They're in the first network, the Drive Bay and the whole Midrange line.
  • Memory Dies need Doped Silicon (a wafer and redstone), so they too are made from vanilla materials. Many of the things built with them also need neodymium or tantalum: larger dies and drives, ports and modules, the Gateway. Schematic Cards and Job Buffers don't.
  • Processor Dies need gallium. They're in the Fabricator, the Scheduler, the Schematic Encoder, the 2M die and the servers.

The 8K Storage Die starts the storage tiers; the larger ones are crafted from it (see Storage Dies).

The recipe type#

Press recipes are their own recipe type, encodedlogistics:lithography. With JEI installed they have their own category, with the press as its catalyst (see JEI). Every lithography recipe is listed on the recipe reference.

For pack makers, a recipe names a wafer, an additive and a photomask (each an ingredient), a result, and optionally energy (FE, default 4000) and time (ticks, default 100):

{
  "type": "encodedlogistics:lithography",
  "wafer": "encodedlogistics:silicon_wafer",
  "additive": "minecraft:redstone",
  "photomask": "encodedlogistics:logic_photomask",
  "result": { "id": "encodedlogistics:logic_die", "count": 1 },
  "energy": 4000,
  "time": 100
}
