Crafting and automation

The mod's materials from ore to Processor Die, and autocrafting - schematics, the machines that carry them out, the Schedulers that run jobs, and the early Midrange line.

This section has two halves. The first is making things: the mod's three ores, the fabrication chain that turns sand, iron and copper into Circuit Substrate and Silicon Wafers, and the Lithography Press that etches those into the dies nearly every device is built from. The second is autocrafting: teaching the network recipes and letting it craft for you.

Materials#

  1. Ores and metals: neodymium, tantalum and gallium, where they generate, and their ingots and blocks.
  2. Fabrication materials: Ferrite, Copper Foil, Solder Paste, Circuit Substrate, silicon and the other parts of the chain.
  3. Lithography: the Lithography Press, photomasks, and the Logic, Memory and Processor Dies.

The dies and metals set the pace of the mod: Logic and Memory Dies are made from vanilla materials, most of what's built with Memory Dies also needs neodymium or tantalum, and Processor Dies need gallium. Progression follows that path.

Autocrafting#

Autocrafting on a network has three parts:

PartWhat it doesWhere
A schematicA recipe written on a card: what goes in, what comes outSchematics
A providerA machine holding schematics that carries them out: a Fabricator crafts, a Gateway runs other mods' machinesFabricators and Gateways
A SchedulerPlans and runs jobs: works out the steps, holds the ingredients, hands each step to a providerSchedulers

Then you ask for something from any terminal and watch it happen: Crafting jobs.

Before any of that (before Processor Dies), the Midrange line gives you a smaller autocrafting system from early materials: recipes punched on cards, read onto diskettes, and crafted by a Midrange System. See The Midrange line.
