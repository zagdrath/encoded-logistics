Getting started

Where to begin with Encoded Logistics, and the order to read the guides in.

Encoded Logistics starts with vanilla materials and a supply of FE, and ends with racks of servers running autocrafting jobs and scripts. These two pages get you going:

  1. Your first network: from sand and iron to a working storage network with a terminal. It covers the first silicon, the Lithography Press, the first dies, and then the six blocks and items every network starts with.
  2. Progression: the tech path after that, tier by tier, with what each new material unlocks and where it's documented.

Before you begin#

  • Bring your own power. The mod has no generator. The Lithography Press and every network run on FE from another mod: a generator, a solar panel or anything else that pushes FE into a block. A Creative Capacitor Bank (creative mode only) stands in for one while you try things out.
  • Install JEI if you can. Every recipe on these pages is also in JEI, including the Lithography Press's own recipe category. See JEI.
  • Hold Shift on an item. Most of the mod's blocks and items have a short description in their tooltip.

The ideas in one paragraph#

A network is everything joined by cables to one Network Controller structure. The controller buffers energy and hands out lanes: each device (a Drive Bay, a terminal, a port) needs a lane to work, and each cable carries only so many. Drives in Drive Bays are the network's storage, and terminals let you browse it. When you want the details, read Networks next.
