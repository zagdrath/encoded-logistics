Server racks

The Server Rack and the rack devices that go in it - what a rack is for, and where to read about each part of it.

Most of the network's advanced hardware doesn't go in the world as blocks: it goes in a Server Rack. A rack is a 42U enclosure two blocks deep and three tall. You fill it with rack devices - switches, routers, servers, storage arrays, tape libraries, power and security - each taking 1 to 10 units of height. The rack is one device on the network, and the devices in it share its connection.
