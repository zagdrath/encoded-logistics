CHGNBR

Command line and programs Firewall: BUILD

Change Neighbour

Syntax

CHGNBR RTR(device name) NBR(name (up to 10 characters)) [COST(*DFT | whole number)]

The first 3 parameters can be given without their keyword, in that order: RTR, NBR, COST.

Parameters

KeywordPromptTakesRequiredDefaultSpecial values
RTRRouterDevice name
prompter field 10 long; F4 lists devices		Yes
NBRNeighbourName (up to 10 characters)
prompter field 10 long		Yes
COSTCost overrideWhole number
1 to 1,000,000		No*DFT*DFT

Sets the cost a Router gives the hop to one of its neighbours, overriding the cost worked out from the link. Routes are chosen by lowest total cost, so a higher cost steers traffic away from that neighbour and a lower one towards it. RTR and NBR are as RTVNBRSTS takes them (ELC2601, ELC2605).

COST(n) sets the override (1 to 1,000,000); COST(*DFT) removes it, so the worked-out cost applies again. The Router works its paths out again with the new cost, which may change routes (a changed best path raises the *RTECHG trigger event). On success the command sends ELC2609.

CHGNBR RTR(ROUTER01) NBR(ELNET03) COST(5000)
CHGNBR RTR(ROUTER01) NBR(ELNET05) COST(*DFT)
