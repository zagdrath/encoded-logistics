Move Item

Syntax

MOVITM ITEM(item id) QTY(*ALL | whole number) TODEV(*DESK | device name) [PARTIAL(*NO | *YES)] [RTNMOVED(*INT variable)] [TYPE(*ITEM | *FLUID | *PRES | *ALL)]

The first 3 parameters can be given without their keyword, in that order: ITEM, QTY, TODEV.

Takes an item, fluid or gas out of the network's storage and puts it into the inventory a device faces, or into the drawer of the Terminal Desk you're working at.

Where it goes#

  • TODEV(name): a cable part on the network, usually an Egress Port or Ingress Port (or an Inventory Tap), by its device name (EGRESS01). The items go into the block the part faces, through the side the part is on. A device that isn't a cable part is ELC1303; a part that's offline or disabled is ELC1302; one that faces no inventory is ELC1304. A qualified SYSNAME/DEVICE name reaches a published device on another network (see RTVDIRENT).
  • TODEV(*DESK): the drawer of the Terminal Desk running the interactive job. In a batch job, or anywhere without a desk, *DESK is ELC1301.

How much#

QTY(n) moves up to n; QTY(*ALL) everything the network has, hot and cold. With PARTIAL(*YES) (the default) it moves what it can (what there is, and what fits) and says how much in RTNMOVED, with no error for a shortfall. With PARTIAL(*NO) it fails with ELC1202 before moving anything when the network has fewer than you asked for, and also afterwards when fewer fitted; in that second case the ones that did fit stay moved, and RTNMOVED says how many.

The item ID forms (bare COAL or qualified 'minecraft:coal') are the ones RTVITMCNT describes; an unknown ID, or one the network holds none of, is ELC1201. The command sends no completion message (only ELC1203 when it recalls from tape).

Items on tape#

When there aren't enough hot items and more are on tape, the command asks the Tape Libraries to recall what's missing and sends ELC1203. In a program, the job then waits (using none of its command budget) until enough of the item is hot again or no recall of it is left, and then moves what's there. Typed at the ===> prompt, it starts the recall and moves what's hot now: run it again once the tape has been read. See LTO tapes and Tape Libraries.

Fluids and gases#

With TYPE(*FLUID) or TYPE(*PRES) the ID is a fluid's or gas's full ID ('minecraft:lava') and QTY is in mB (or the gas's mod's unit). It goes into the faced block's tanks (ELC1208 when the block has none for that type) or, with *DESK, into empty containers in the drawer, such as buckets. Fluids and gases are never on tape. Naming one of the other type is ELC1207.

DCL VAR(&MOVED) TYPE(*INT)
MOVITM ITEM(COAL) QTY(256) TODEV(EGRESS01) RTNMOVED(&MOVED)
IF COND(&MOVED *LT 256) THEN(SNDPGMMSG MSG('Furnace feed short:' *BCAT %CHAR(&MOVED)))
MOVITM ITEM('minecraft:lava') QTY(4000) TODEV(EGRESS02) TYPE(*FLUID)
MOVITM ITEM(DIAMOND) QTY(9) TODEV(EGRESS03) PARTIAL(*NO)
MONMSG MSGID(ELC1202) EXEC(SNDPGMMSG MSG('Not 9 diamonds to spare'))
