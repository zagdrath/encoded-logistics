DSPMSG

Command line only Firewall: None

Display Messages

Syntax

DSPMSG [USR(*CURRENT | *SYSOPR | name (up to 10 characters))]

The first parameter can be given without its keyword, in that order: USR.

Parameters

KeywordPromptTakesRequiredDefaultSpecial values
USRUserName (up to 10 characters)
prompter field 10 long		No*CURRENT*CURRENT, *SYSOPR

DSPMSG opens Display Messages, the screen for a message queue: what your jobs, the system and other users sent you with SNDMSG. It's also option 7 on the main menu.

                                                                Day 2  07:14:22
 Queue . . . . :   ZAGDRATH                 Program . . . . :   *DSPMSG
 Type options, press Enter.
   4=Remove   5=Display details

Opt  Sev  From        Sent             Message
     30   RESTOCK     Day 2  07:10:44  Called program ZAGLIB/RESTOCK ended abno
     00   UPSALERT    Day 2  06:58:02  Utility power restored. UPS charge 82%.
     10   QSYSOPR     Day 2  06:40:15  Storage 91% used (hot tier).

                                       Newest first; unread messages are bright.








 Parameters or command                                                   Bottom
 ===>

 F1=Help  F3=Exit  F5=Refresh  F11=Remove all  F12=Cancel  F24=More keys

Messages are listed newest first, with their severity, who sent them (a user, a program or QSYS), when, and as much of the text as fits. Unread messages are bright; showing them marks them read, and MW goes from the message line.

Option or keyDoes
4Removes the message, straight away
5Shows its details in a window: the whole text, its message ID, severity, sender and time
F5Refreshes the list
F11Removes every message in the queue, after you confirm with Enter (F12 backs out)

Another user's queue#

USR(*CURRENT) (the default) or your own name shows your own queue. Any other name shows that user's queue, and USR(*SYSOPR) (or USR(QSYSOPR)) the system operator's, where SNDMSG TOUSR(*SYSOPR) messages go. Looking at someone else's queue needs one of:

  • being a *SECOFR: the network's owner (the Firewall's), or a server operator;
  • full authority on the system: a network with no Firewall, or the SECLVL system value at 10 (see users and security).

Otherwise the screen shows ELC0401. Messages you read or remove there are read or removed for that user.

DSPMSG
DSPMSG USR(*SYSOPR)
DSPMSG USR(ZAGDRATH)

See the Terminal OS screens and Jobs, scheduling and triggers for message queues.
