UPDDTA

Command line only Firewall: None

Update Data

Syntax

UPDDTA FILE(library/name)

The first parameter can be given without its keyword, in that order: FILE.

Parameters

KeywordPromptTakesRequiredDefaultSpecial values
FILEFileLibrary/name
prompter field 21 long; F4 lists files		Yes

UPDDTA opens Update Data: a physical file's records one at a time, to change, delete or add, as a midrange data file utility does. It's 2=Change data on Work with Files (WRKF). Without a library, the file is looked for down your library list.

 UPDDTA                           Update Data                  System:  ELNET01
                                                                Day 2  07:14:22
 File  . . . . . :   ZAGLIB/STOCK               Mode  . . . :   CHANGE
 Format  . . . . :   STOCKREC                   Record  . . :   2 of 3
 Position to key . . .

 Type changes, press Enter.

 ITEM       Item . . . . . . . . . . : GOLD_INGOT           20A
 QTY        Quantity . . . . . . . . : 3          9S 0
 COST       Unit cost  . . . . . . . : 9.99      7P 2
 HOT         . . . . . . . . . . . . : 1 1L
 SEEN       Last counted . . . . . . : 00003 07:00:00 T










 F1=Help F3=Exit F5=Refresh F6=Add F7=Previous F8=Next F11=Delete F12=Cancel

Each field is a row: its name, its text or column heading, its value in an input field, and its definition (64A, 9P 2). Fields that don't fit one screen go on further pages (PageUp / PageDown).

  • Change mode shows a record. Change its values and press Enter to save them; Enter with nothing changed goes on to the next record. F7 / F8 show the record before or after. F11 deletes the record, after you confirm. On a keyed file, type a key in Position to and press Enter to go to the first record at or after it.
  • Entry mode (F6; an empty file opens in it) is for new records: type the values and press Enter to add one; the fields clear for the next. F10 goes back to change mode.

Values are checked by type before they're sent: a whole number for S, a number for P, 1 or 0 for L, and for T a game timestamp (Day 12 06:30) or blank for the moment it's written. The server checks them again: a value that doesn't fit (ELC2209) or a duplicate UNIQUE key (ELC2203) shows on the message line, with the cursor on the field it names. A record whose values come to more than about 8,000 characters in all can't be sent from the screen.

Changing records needs *CHANGE on the file's library (ELC0401). ELSYS's files are the network's own data and read-only: UPDDTA on one is refused with ELC0205 (use DSPPFM to look at them). See Screens and the database screens.

UPDDTA FILE(ELGPL/ITEMHIST)
