SNDDSPGPH

Command line and programs Firewall: None

Send Display Graph

Syntax

SNDDSPGPH DEV(device name) RGN(name (up to 10 characters)) STAT(*ITEMFLOW | *ENERGY | *LANES | *STORAGE | *CRAFTING | *ITEM | *MCHOPS | *MCHFE) [ITEM(*NONE | item id)] [RANGE(*1M | *10M | *1H | *1D)] [TYPE(*LINE | *BAR)] [COLOR(*DFT | *BLUE | *BROWN | *CYAN | *DARK | *FOREST | *GREEN | *GREY | *LIGHT | *MINT | *NAVY | *ORANGE | *RED | *RUST | *SILVER | *STEEL | *WHITE | *YELLOW | text)]

The first 3 parameters can be given without their keyword, in that order: DEV, RGN, STAT.

SNDDSPGPH puts a graph in a region of a Display Panel screen: one statistic over a time range, as a line or bar chart. It replaces whatever the region showed. A Text screen given a graph becomes a Dashboard. The region must exist (CHGDSPRGN; on a screen with none set up, A is the whole screen), or it's ELC1314.

*MCHOPS and *MCHFE graph a machine bridged through Arcforge: give the machine's device name in ITEM, for example ITEM(FURN01). A name that isn't on the network is ELC1301, and a device that isn't such a machine ELC1318. *ITEM, *MCHOPS and *MCHFE without an ITEM are ELC1316, and so is an ITEM given to any other statistic.

Where the numbers come from#

  • With a Monitoring Server online on the network, *ITEMFLOW, *ENERGY, *LANES, *CRAFTING, *MCHOPS and *MCHFE come from its records, over the whole RANGE.
  • Otherwise, and always for *STORAGE and *ITEM, the screen keeps its own history: one sample a second while it's loaded, for the last rack.displayHistorySeconds (default 600) seconds. RANGE(*1M) shows the last minute of it; the longer ranges show all of it there is so far.

RANGE is *1M, *10M (the default), *1H or *1D, and TYPE is *LINE (the default) or *BAR. A graph has at most 256 points; a longer series is averaged down to fit. COLOR is the line or bar colour: *DFT, a palette name such as *CYAN, or '#RRGGBB' in quotes.

The screen doesn't have to be online. A DEV that isn't on the network is ELC1301, and a device that isn't a Display Panel screen ELC1303. SNDDSPGPH sends no completion message.

Examples#

SNDDSPGPH DEV(DSP01) RGN(A) STAT(*ENERGY) RANGE(*1H)
SNDDSPGPH DEV(DSP01) RGN(B) STAT(*ITEM) ITEM(minecraft:iron_ingot) TYPE(*BAR) COLOR(*ORANGE)
SNDDSPGPH DEV(DSP01) RGN(C) STAT(*MCHOPS) ITEM(FURN01) RANGE(*10M)

See Graphs.
