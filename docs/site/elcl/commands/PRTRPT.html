PRTRPT

Command line and programs Firewall: None

Print Report

Syntax

PRTRPT RPT(*INV | *DEV | *JOBLOG | *SPLF) [SPLF(*LAST | name (up to 10 characters))] [DEV(*DFT | device name)]

The first parameter can be given without its keyword, in that order: RPT.

Parameters

KeywordPromptTakesRequiredDefaultSpecial values
RPTReportSpecial valueYes*INV, *DEV, *JOBLOG, *SPLF
SPLFSpooled fileName (up to 10 characters)
prompter field 10 long		No*LAST*LAST
DEVPrinterDevice name
prompter field 10 long; F4 lists devices		No*DFT*DFT

PRTRPT prints a report straight to paper on a Line Printer: it comes out of the printer's front as a Printout, one sheet of Paper a page. Nothing is spooled. (To collect lines in a spooled file first, use PRTTXT.)

RPTPrintsTitle on the pages
*INVEvery item in the network's storage: its quantity, tier (hot and cold listed apart) and where most of it is, then the totalNETWORK INVENTORY LISTING
*DEVEvery named device: its name, type, status, lanes and where it isDEVICE LIST
*JOBLOGThis job's log: the time, the job and each event (commands, and messages with their IDs; errors in red)JOB LOG - and the job
*SPLFOne of this job's spooled files, as it isThe spooled file's name
  • *JOBLOG and *SPLF use the job the command runs in: the batch job, or your interactive job on the command line. A job log is only ever printed this way; it's never spooled.
  • With *SPLF, SPLF(*LAST) (the default) is the job's newest spooled file, and a name its newest file of that name, such as QPRINT for PRTTXT's. A file the job doesn't have is ELC0103 for SPLF. Print another job's files from Work with Output (WRKSPLF, 6=Print).
  • *INV needs the network's storage: without it the command stops with ELC1302 for *NETWORK.

The printer#

DEV(*DFT) (the default) is the first online Line Printer on the network; with none, ELC1301 names PRT01. DEV(name) picks one by its device name: not there is ELC1301, offline ELC1302, and a printer with no paper at all ELC1306.

A page holds 42 lines of 56 characters, under a header with the system, the title and the date, and a page number; a longer line carries on onto the next. A report is at most 100 pages. If the paper runs out part-way, the pages printed so far come out and the rest wait in the printer, printing as soon as you put more paper in.

When it has printed, PRTRPT sends ELC1307 with the report's title and the printer's name.

PRTRPT RPT(*INV)
PRTRPT RPT(*DEV) DEV(PRT02)
PRTRPT RPT(*JOBLOG)
PRTRPT RPT(*SPLF) SPLF(QPRINT)

The Line Printer's own screen prints the same reports by hand. See Jobs, scheduling and triggers for spooled output.
