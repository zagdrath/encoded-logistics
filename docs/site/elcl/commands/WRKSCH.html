WRKSCH

Command line only Firewall: None

Work with Schematics

Syntax

WRKSCH [HOLDER(*ALL | name (up to 10 characters))]

The first parameter can be given without its keyword, in that order: HOLDER.

Parameters

KeywordPromptTakesRequiredDefaultSpecial values
HOLDERHolderName (up to 10 characters)
prompter field 10 long		No*ALL*ALL

WRKSCH opens Work with Schematics: the network's schematic holders (Fabricators, Gateways, Fabrication Servers and Midrange Systems) and the schematics in them, a row each, with what each makes, how many, whether it's a crafting or a processing schematic, and where the holder is. A holder with no schematics gets a row of its own. You type it at a command line; it doesn't run in a program.

  • HOLDER(*ALL), the default, lists every holder. Give a holder's device name, or the start of its name, to see only that one. A name that matches nothing gives an empty list.
  • A Midrange System's schematics are its diskettes' recipes, marked R/O: they can be shown but not removed.
OptionWhat it does
4=RemoveTakes the schematic out of its holder into the Terminal Desk's drawer, after you confirm (ELC2504). It needs the Firewall's build permission and a Terminal Desk, and room in its drawer.
5=DisplayWhat the schematic makes and from what.
8=LocateOutlines its holder in the world for ten seconds; its coordinates show on the message line.

In a program, RTVSCHLST lists schematics. See Schematics and Screens.
