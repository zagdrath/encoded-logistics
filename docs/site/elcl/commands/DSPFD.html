DSPFD

Command line only Firewall: None

Display File Description

Syntax

DSPFD FILE(library/name)

The first parameter can be given without its keyword, in that order: FILE.

Parameters

KeywordPromptTakesRequiredDefaultSpecial values
FILEFileLibrary/name
prompter field 21 long; F4 lists files		Yes

DSPFD opens Display File Description: everything about a physical file except its records. It's 8=Display description on Work with Files (WRKF). Without a library, the file is looked for down your library list.

 DSPFD                      Display File Description           System:  ELNET01
                                                                Day 2  07:14:22
 File  . . . . . . . . . . . :  ZAGLIB/STOCK
 Attribute . . . . . . . . . :  PF
 Text  . . . . . . . . . . . :  Stock levels
 Record format . . . . . . . :  STOCKREC   Stock record
 Source member . . . . . . . :  ZAGLIB/STOCK   (changed since the file was made)
 Created . . . . . . . . . . :  Day 2  07:20
 Records . . . . . . . . . . :  3   Limit  10,000
 Record length . . . . . . . :  51
 Storage, bytes  . . . . . . :  3
 Key . . . . . . . . . . . . :  ITEM   Unique

 Field       Type  Length  Dec  Key  Text
 ITEM        A         20         1  Item
 QTY         S          9    0       Quantity
 COST        P          7    2       Unit cost
 HOT         L          1
 SEEN        T         14            Last counted

 Press Enter to continue.


 F1=Help  F3=Exit  F5=Refresh  F12=Cancel

It shows the file's library, attribute and text; its record format; the source member it was made from (or *NONE for a file made by RUNQRY or CPYF, *SYSTEM for ELSYS's system files) and whether that member changed since; when it was made; how many records it holds of the elcl.maxRecordsPerFile limit; its record length and the storage the records take; and its key. Then a line for each field: name, type (A character, S integer, P decimal, L logical, T game timestamp), length, decimals, its place in the key, and its text or column heading.

PageUp / PageDown roll it; Enter or F12 goes back. Use it to check a field's exact name and type before writing a program (DCLF) or a query. See Screens.

DSPFD FILE(ELSYS/INVITEMS)
