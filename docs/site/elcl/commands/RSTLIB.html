RSTLIB

Command line and programs Firewall: None

Restore Library

Syntax

RSTLIB LIB(name (up to 10 characters)) DEV(device name)

The first 2 parameters can be given without their keyword, in that order: LIB, DEV.

Parameters

KeywordPromptTakesRequiredDefaultSpecial values
LIBLibraryName (up to 10 characters)
prompter field 10 long		Yes
DEVDeviceDevice name
prompter field 10 long; F4 lists devices		Yes

RSTLIB restores a library saved with SAVLIB from an 8" Diskette, on this system or any other. DEV is the diskette drive (a Midrange System, Integrated Midrange System or Card Reader, by device name): it reads the first diskette in that drive holding a library of that name. The library comes back under the same name; there's no way to restore it under another.

  • A new library is created, owned by you, with public authority *USE, and its type and text from the diskette.
  • An existing library has its contents replaced: its members, programs and files become exactly the diskette's, so members that aren't on the diskette are deleted. That takes the right to change the library (ELC0401 otherwise), and ELSYS can never be restored over (ELC0205).

The members and files must fit in the network's storage, counting what the replaced ones took (ELC0207 otherwise), and no member may be longer than elcl.maxSourceLines (default 5000) lines (ELC0004). Nothing is changed when either check fails.

Messages#

  • ELC0221: restored, naming the device and the diskette's label.
  • ELC0201: no diskette in the drive holds that library.
  • ELC1301: no device of that name; ELC1302: it's offline (a Midrange System that isn't running); ELC1310: no diskette in it.
RSTLIB LIB(ZAGLIB) DEV(CARDRDR01)

See Saving to diskette.

System values, save and restore

WRKSYSVAL RTVSYSVAL CHGSYSVAL SAVLIB
