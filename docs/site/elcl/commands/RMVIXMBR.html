RMVIXMBR

Command line and programs Firewall: BUILD

Remove Exchange Member

Syntax

RMVIXMBR IX(name (up to 10 characters)) NETWORK(name (up to 10 characters))

The first 2 parameters can be given without their keyword, in that order: IX, NETWORK.

Parameters

KeywordPromptTakesRequiredDefaultSpecial values
IXExchangeName (up to 10 characters)
prompter field 10 long		Yes
NETWORKNetworkName (up to 10 characters)
prompter field 10 long		Yes

Removes a member network from an Exchange Switch, or turns down a request to join, as WRKIX's 4=Remove does. Only the exchange's owner can do it from a command (who counts as the owner is as for APVIXMBR; anyone else gets ELC2612); a member can also leave from its own side, in the panel. IX and NETWORK are as APVIXMBR takes them (ELC2610, ELC2611).

The member's port is freed and its sessions with the other members end. On success the command sends ELC2615. See Exchange Switch and Directory Server.

RMVIXMBR IX(XSW01) NETWORK(ELNET07)
