SNDDSPTXT

Command line and programs Firewall: None

Send Display Text

Syntax

SNDDSPTXT DEV(device name) TEXT(text) [LINE(*NEXT | whole number)] [CLEAR(*NO | *YES)] [SIZE(*SAME | *SMALL | *NORMAL | *LARGE | *HUGE)] [COLOR(*SAME | *DFT | *BLUE | *BROWN | *CYAN | *DARK | *FOREST | *GREEN | *GREY | *LIGHT | *MINT | *NAVY | *ORANGE | *RED | *RUST | *SILVER | *STEEL | *WHITE | *YELLOW | text)] [ALIGN(*SAME | *LEFT | *CENTER | *RIGHT)]

The first 2 parameters can be given without their keyword, in that order: DEV, TEXT.

SNDDSPTXT writes a line of text on a Display Panel screen, named by its device name (DSP01). In Text mode the lines fill the whole screen; in Dashboard mode they show in any region whose widget is *TEXT (see SNDDSPWDG). Writing text doesn't change the screen's mode.

The screen must be online: offline (no running network, or no lane) is ELC1302. A device that isn't a Display Panel screen is ELC1303.

Where the line goes#

  • LINE(n) writes line n, 1 being the top, replacing what was there; empty lines are added above it if needed. The highest line is the number of *SMALL lines the screen holds, its canvas height over 6 pixels (5 on a screen one panel high, 10 on two, 32 on six). Past that is ELC0004.
  • LINE(*NEXT) (the default) writes the line after the last one written: the top line on a new or cleared screen, line 4 after a LINE(3). When the lines no longer fit down the screen, the top ones roll off, so a run of *NEXT writes scrolls like a log.
  • CLEAR(*YES) removes every text line first, and *NEXT starts at the top again. Regions and widgets are left alone; CLRDSP clears those.

Text longer than fits across the screen at the line's size is cut. A line holds the screen's canvas width less 8 pixels of margin, over the character width: 4 pixels at *SMALL, 6 at *NORMAL, 12 at *LARGE, 18 at *HUGE. A screen four panels wide (128 pixels) holds 30, 20, 10 or 6 characters; one panel wide, only 4 at *NORMAL.

Size, colour and alignment#

Each line keeps its own size, colour and alignment. *SAME, the default for all three, keeps what the line had; a new line starts at *NORMAL, the default text colour and *LEFT.

ParameterValues
SIZE*SMALL (a 4 x 6 font), *NORMAL (the 6 x 10 terminal font), *LARGE (twice that), *HUGE (three times)
COLOR*DFT (the default text colour), a palette name such as *MINT, *RED or *YELLOW, or '#RRGGBB' in quotes. Anything else is ELC0003
ALIGN*LEFT, *CENTER, *RIGHT

The palette's names are listed on Display Panels. SNDDSPTXT sends no completion message.

Examples#

SNDDSPTXT DEV(DSP01) TEXT('PLANT STATUS') LINE(1) CLEAR(*YES) +
          SIZE(*LARGE) COLOR(*MINT) ALIGN(*CENTER)
SNDDSPTXT DEV(DSP01) TEXT('Power: mains') LINE(2) COLOR(*GREEN)
SNDDSPTXT DSP01 'Restock started'                   /* the next line */
SNDDSPTXT DSP02 'ALARM' LINE(1) COLOR('#FF4040') SIZE(*HUGE)

RTVDSPSIZ tells a program how big the screen is. See Display Panels.
