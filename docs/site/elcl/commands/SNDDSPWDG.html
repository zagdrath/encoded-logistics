SNDDSPWDG

Command line and programs Firewall: None

Send Display Widget

Syntax

SNDDSPWDG DEV(device name) RGN(name (up to 10 characters)) WDG(*STORAGE | *COLD | *ENERGY | *LANES | *JOBS | *ITEM | *CLOCK | *DEVICES | *UPS | *TEXT | *TABLE | *NONE) [ITEM(*NONE | item id)] [DEVTYPE(*ALL | name (up to 10 characters))] [COLOR(*DFT | *BLUE | *BROWN | *CYAN | *DARK | *FOREST | *GREEN | *GREY | *LIGHT | *MINT | *NAVY | *ORANGE | *RED | *RUST | *SILVER | *STEEL | *WHITE | *YELLOW | text)] [FILE(*NONE | library/name)] [QRYSLT(*ALL | text)] [SORT(*NONE | *ASCEND | *DESCEND | name (up to 10 characters))]

The first 3 parameters can be given without their keyword, in that order: DEV, RGN, WDG.

SNDDSPWDG puts a widget in a region of a Display Panel screen: a live gauge, a list, a clock, the screen's text lines or a table of a file's records. It replaces whatever the region showed (a graph or an image too). A Text screen given a widget becomes a Dashboard. The region must exist (set it up with CHGDSPRGN; on a screen with none set up, A is the whole screen); one that doesn't is ELC1314.

The widgets#

WDGShowsNeeds
*STORAGEHot storage: how full the network's drives are, as a percentage and a bar
*COLDCold storage: how full the online Tape Libraries are
*ENERGYThe network's energy draw in FE/t, with a bar of its stored energy
*LANESLanes in use out of the network's total
*JOBSThe active crafting jobs: how many, and a row each with its number, item and how far along it is
*ITEMOne item's count in the network's storageITEM()
*CLOCKThe game time and day
*DEVICESThe network's devices with their status, all of them or one typeDEVTYPE() (optional)
*UPSThe UPSes: mains or battery, their average charge, and a row each
*TEXTThe screen's text lines, written with SNDDSPTXT
*TABLEA file's records, selected and sortedFILE(), QRYSLT(), SORT()
*NONENothing: the region is empty

Live widgets (all but *CLOCK, *TEXT and *NONE) are worked out on the server once a second, for the players near the screen. For graphs use SNDDSPGPH, and for pictures SNDDSPIMG.

Data sources#

Each source parameter belongs to one widget. Giving it to another widget is ELC1316, and so is leaving out one a widget needs.

  • ITEM names the item for *ITEM, as other commands take it (LOGIC_DIE, minecraft:iron_ingot). An item the game doesn't have is ELC1201, and a name more than one mod uses ELC1205.
  • DEVTYPE limits *DEVICES to one device type, as Work with Devices shows it (LGT, SPK, DSP...); *ALL (the default) lists every device.
  • FILE, QRYSLT and SORT make a *TABLE, and work as RUNQRY's: the file (*LIBL searched when it isn't qualified; not found: ELC2205), a selection expression over its fields (*ALL: every record) and the sort fields, each optionally followed by *DESCEND (up to 8 values in all). The selection and sort are checked against the file now: a name that isn't a field is ELC2242. The table shows the headings, then a row a record, as many as fit and at most 64. If the file goes away later, the region shows ! and the message instead. QRYSLT and SORT are ignored by the other widgets.

COLOR is the widget's accent colour: *DFT (its own), a palette name such as *MINT, or '#RRGGBB' in quotes.

The screen doesn't have to be online. A name that isn't on the network is ELC1301, and a device that isn't a Display Panel screen ELC1303. SNDDSPWDG sends no completion message.

Examples#

SNDDSPWDG DEV(DSP01) RGN(A) WDG(*STORAGE) COLOR(*MINT)
SNDDSPWDG DEV(DSP01) RGN(B) WDG(*ITEM) ITEM(minecraft:iron_ingot)
SNDDSPWDG DEV(DSP01) RGN(C) WDG(*DEVICES) DEVTYPE(UPS)
SNDDSPWDG DEV(DSP02) RGN(A) WDG(*TABLE) FILE(ELSYS/DEVICES) +
          QRYSLT('STATUS *NE "*ONLINE"') SORT(TYPE NAME)
SNDDSPWDG DEV(DSP02) RGN(B) WDG(*TEXT)

See Widgets.
