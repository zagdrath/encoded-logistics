CHGDSPRGN

Command line and programs Firewall: None

Change Display Region

Syntax

CHGDSPRGN DEV(device name) RGN(name (up to 10 characters)) [X(*SAME | whole number)] [Y(*SAME | whole number)] [W(*SAME | whole number)] [H(*SAME | whole number)] [BG(*SAME | *DFT | text)]

The first 2 parameters can be given without their keyword, in that order: DEV, RGN.

CHGDSPRGN defines a region on a Display Panel screen, or moves, resizes or recolours one. A region is a named rectangle of the screen's canvas (32 pixels a panel each way, from the top left) that shows one widget. A Text screen that a program gives a region becomes a Dashboard.

A new region or an old one#

RGN names the region: 1-8 letters and digits (anything else is ELC0003), in any case.

  • An existing region keeps whatever you leave as *SAME, and keeps its widget.
  • A new region starts at X(0) Y(0) and runs to the right and bottom edges of the screen, unless you give its position and size. It has no widget until SNDDSPWDG, SNDDSPGPH or SNDDSPIMG gives it one.

The region must lie on the screen, and must not overlap any other region; either way it's ELC1314 and nothing changes.

Note

A screen with no regions set up has one region, A, covering all of it. A new region would overlap it, so make A smaller first, then add the others in the space you freed.

Background#

BG is the region's background colour: *SAME (the default) keeps it (none, on a new region), *DFT is no background of its own, and a palette name or #RRGGBB sets one. Palette names and hex colours go in quotes here: BG('*NAVY'), BG('#1F2228') (an unquoted BG(*NAVY) is refused with ELC0103). A value that isn't a colour is ELC0003.

A region that changes size has its image, if it shows one, drawn again at the new size. The screen doesn't have to be online. A name that isn't on the network is ELC1301, and a device that isn't a Display Panel screen ELC1303. CHGDSPRGN sends no completion message.

Examples#

A 4 x 2 screen (128 x 64 pixels), split into a left half and two quarters on the right:

CHGDSPRGN DEV(DSP01) RGN(A) W(64)                       /* A: the left half      */
CHGDSPRGN DEV(DSP01) RGN(B) X(64) H(32) BG('*NAVY')    /* B: top right          */
CHGDSPRGN DEV(DSP01) RGN(C) X(64) Y(32)                 /* C: the rest, to the edges */

Use RTVDSPSIZ to work the sizes out from the screen's real size. See Regions.

Messages, displays and output
