WRKCRFJOB

Command line only Firewall: None

Work with Crafting Jobs

Syntax

WRKCRFJOB

It takes no parameters.

WRKCRFJOB opens Work with Jobs: the network's crafting jobs, active and queued, with each one's item, quantity, status (Active, Waiting, Recall), progress bar and scheduler. It refreshes every two seconds. It's Main Menu option 2. You type it at a command line; it doesn't run in a program. (Script jobs are on Work with Active Jobs, WRKACTJOB.)

OptionWhat it does
4=CancelPress Enter again, with no other option typed, to confirm; F5 or another option forgets it. Needs the Firewall's craft permission.
5=DisplayThe job: its item and quantity, status, progress, scheduler, and what it's waiting for.
8=Display stepsThe job's step tree: each step's state, and a stalled step's reason. A job not planned yet has none (ELC2503).

F10 switches to the jobs that ended (newest first; the network keeps the last CRFLOGRTN of them, a system value: see WRKSYSVAL), with their status (Done, Failed, Cancelled), when they ended, how long they took and who asked. There, 4=Remove deletes records (confirmed), 5=Display shows a record (what it made, why it failed, who asked, the ingredients used) and 7=Craft again opens Craft Item with the same item and quantity. F11 sorts by ended time, item or status, and Position to keeps the items that start with what you type. F10 again goes back.

In a program, RTVCRFSTS and ENDCRAFT work on jobs, and ELSYS/CRFHIST holds the history as records. See Crafting jobs and Screens.
