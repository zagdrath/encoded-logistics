RTVCRFSTS

Command line and programs Firewall: VIEW

Retrieve Craft Status

Syntax

RTVCRFSTS CRFJOB(name (up to 10 characters)) [RTNSTS(*CHAR variable)] [RTNPCT(*DEC variable)]

The first parameter can be given without its keyword, in that order: CRFJOB.

Parameters

KeywordPromptTakesRequiredDefaultSpecial values
CRFJOBCraft jobName (up to 10 characters)
prompter field 10 long		Yes
RTNSTSReturn statusA *CHAR variable the command setsNo
RTNPCTReturn percent doneA *DEC variable the command setsNo

Tells you where a crafting job has got to. CRFJOB takes the ID STRCRAFT returned (C0042) or just its number (42).

VariableA job still on a SchedulerA job that has ended
RTNSTS*ACTIVE (running) or *QUEUED*DONE, *FAILED or *CANCELLED
RTNPCTHow much of the job is done, as a percentage100 when done; otherwise what it made as a percentage of what was asked

An ended job is found in the network's crafting history, which keeps the last CRFLOGRTN jobs (a system value; its default is elcl.craftLogRetention (default 200)). A job that has aged out of it, or a number no job has had, is ELC1404; so is an ID that isn't a number. The command sends no completion message.

DCL VAR(&STS) TYPE(*CHAR) LEN(10)
DCL VAR(&PCT) TYPE(*DEC) LEN(5 1)
RTVCRFSTS CRFJOB(C0042) RTNSTS(&STS) RTNPCT(&PCT)
MONMSG MSGID(ELC1404) EXEC(CHGVAR VAR(&STS) VALUE('*GONE'))
SNDPGMMSG MSG('C0042:' *BCAT &STS *BCAT %CHAR(&PCT) *TCAT '%')

Crafting

STRCRAFT RTVCRFLOG ENDCRAFT RTVSCHLST RTVCRFSTP
