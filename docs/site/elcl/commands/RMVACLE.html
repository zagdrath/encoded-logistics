RMVACLE

Command line and programs Firewall: BUILD

Remove ACL Entry

Syntax

RMVACLE RTR(device name) ACL(name (up to 10 characters)) SEQ(whole number)

The first 3 parameters can be given without their keyword, in that order: RTR, ACL, SEQ.

Parameters

KeywordPromptTakesRequiredDefaultSpecial values
RTRRouterDevice name
prompter field 10 long; F4 lists devices		Yes
ACLACLName (up to 10 characters)
prompter field 10 long		Yes
SEQSequence numberWhole number
1 to 9999		Yes

Removes one rule from a Router's ACL, as WRKACL's 4=Remove does. RTR is the Router's device name (ELC2601 when it isn't a Router on your network), ACL the list's name (ELC2606 when the Router has no such ACL) and SEQ the rule's number as WRKACL shows it (ELC2607 when there's no rule with that number).

The other rules keep their numbers. Removing the last rule leaves the ACL empty, still applied wherever it was: with no rules, everything goes by the default, rack.aclDefaultPermit. The command doesn't delete the ACL itself (the Router's panel does) and sends no completion message.

RMVACLE RTR(ROUTER01) ACL(NOFUEL) SEQ(10)
MONMSG MSGID(ELC2607)
