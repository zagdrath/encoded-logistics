Data components

ComponentSaved with the itemWhat it holds
encodedlogistics:facade_targetsavedThe block a Cable Facade copies the look of. A facade without it is blank.
encodedlogistics:device_namesavedThe name ELCL and Work with Devices know a block device by (CTLIF01, ELDESK01, PLC01, DSP01, LGT01 and so on), kept on the item when the block is broken so it comes back when placed again. Used by the Terminal Desk, Control Interface, PLC, Display Panel, Cage Lights, Alarm Strobes, Speaker, Directory Server, Exchange Switch, the optical blocks and the Midrange line.
encodedlogistics:plc_programsavedA PLC program: its name, source, file formats, who loaded it and with what authority, when it was saved, and its retained variables. Carried by a broken Programmable Logic Controller and a written EEPROM Cartridge.
encodedlogistics:drive_idsavedThe id an item, fluid or pressurized Storage Drive, an LTO tape or a Tape Reel is known by; its contents are saved with the world under this id, not on the item. Given the first time it's mounted.
encodedlogistics:drive_statssavedA drive's or tape's bytes used, total bytes and kinds stored, cached on the item for its tooltip, the drive holders' fill bars and its status light.
encodedlogistics:schematicsavedAn Encoded Schematic's contents: a crafting recipe (Encoded Schematic (Crafting)) or a machine's inputs and outputs (Encoded Schematic (Processing)), written by the Schematic Encoder.
encodedlogistics:punched_recipesavedThe crafting recipe punched onto a Punch Card by the Card Punch. A blank card has none.
encodedlogistics:diskette_recipessavedWhat's written on an 8" Diskette: its label, up to eight recipes read in by the Card Reader, and ELCL libraries saved with SAVLIB. A blank diskette has none.
encodedlogistics:printoutsavedA Printout's pages and where they came from (the printer and when), from the Line Printer.
encodedlogistics:magazine_contentssavedThe 8" Diskettes in a Diskette Magazine (up to four).
encodedlogistics:link_addresssavedWhat a written Link Card holds: a Network Bridge, Point-to-Point Link, segment, network, Wireless Controller or optical device (multiplexer or Transport Shelf), with its position and dimension.
encodedlogistics:handheld_networksavedThe network a Handheld Terminal is linked to.
encodedlogistics:handheld_link_statesavedWhether a Handheld Terminal is unlinked, linked, or out of range of its network, for its icon. Kept up to date while it's in an inventory.
encodedlogistics:wireless_linksavedThe Wireless Controller a Handheld Terminal was linked to, through which it works anywhere.
encodedlogistics:energysavedThe FE in a Handheld Terminal's battery.
encodedlogistics:resource_entrysavedA Resource Entry's fluid or gas and its amount (in mB, or the gas's own unit).
encodedlogistics:drive_energysavedThe FE in an Energy Storage Drive, kept on the item so the drive keeps its charge out of a drive holder and works as a battery.
encodedlogistics:rack_device_statesavedA rack device's settings while it's out of a rack: its configuration, its lane priority if not the default, and its device name. Written when you take the device out, read back when you install it.
encodedlogistics:rack_templatesavedA Rack Elevation's plan of a Server Rack: its name, the devices by unit, and (if those toggles are on) their contents and settings. A blank elevation has none.
