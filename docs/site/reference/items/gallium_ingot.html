Gallium Ingot

encodedlogistics:gallium_ingot · Item · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics: Materials

The top-tier metal: it goes into the Processor Photomask, the 2M Storage Die, Fiber Cable, Optical Transceivers and Radio Modules. Smelted from Raw Gallium, its ore or its dust.
