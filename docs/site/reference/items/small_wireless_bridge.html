Small Wireless Bridge

encodedlogistics:small_wireless_bridge · Item · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics

Goes on a face of an Arcforge machine. Linked with a Link Card from a Wireless Controller, it puts the machine on that network: Work with Machines, ELCL, Gateways and graphs can see and work it, and it can power the machine from the network. Needs Arcforge; sneak-use with an empty hand takes it off.
