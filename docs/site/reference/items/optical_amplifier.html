Optical Amplifier

encodedlogistics:optical_amplifier · Block · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics

An inline housing for a Fiber Cable run (light in at the back, out at the front). While powered it ends the span it's on, so span loss counts again from it; unpowered it just passes the light through. Uses one lane.

Optical Amplifier

Shift: Placed inline on a Fiber Cable run (light in at the back, out at the front), it ends the span it's on while powered, so span loss counts again from it. Unpowered it just passes the light through. Powered by its network, or by an online multiplexer or shelf whose line runs through it.

Works with

  • Fiber Cable
  • Optical Multiplexer
  • Optical Amplifier (Rack)

On a network

It counts as a device of type AMP in Work with Devices and in ELCL; the first one on a network is named AMP01. See Device types.

How to get it

Used to make

  • Amplifier Card
  • Optical Amplifier (Rack)

As a block

Hardness3
Blast resistance6
Toolany pickaxe (needed for it to drop)
Light level0 in its default state

Where it's documented

  • Optical networking (Networking)
