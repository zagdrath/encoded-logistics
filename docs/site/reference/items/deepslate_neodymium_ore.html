Deepslate Neodymium Ore

encodedlogistics:deepslate_neodymium_ore · Block · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics: Materials

Neodymium ore in deepslate, found in the Overworld. Mine it with an iron pickaxe for Raw Neodymium.

How to get it

Mining it drops Raw Neodymium (Silk Touch drops the block itself).

Generates in the Overworld (8 veins per chunk, most common in the middle from Y -32 to Y 48).

Used to make

  • Neodymium Dust
  • Neodymium Ingot

As a block

Hardness4.5
Blast resistance3
Tooliron pickaxe (needed for it to drop)
Light level0 in its default state

#c:ores, #c:ores/neodymium
