Tantalum Dust

encodedlogistics:tantalum_dust · Item · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics: Materials

Tantalum Dust, made only by Arcforge's Arc Crusher from ore, raw metal or ingots (without Arcforge there's no way to make it). Smelt or blast it into an ingot.
