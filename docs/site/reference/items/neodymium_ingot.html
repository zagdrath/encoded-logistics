Neodymium Ingot

encodedlogistics:neodymium_ingot · Item · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics: Materials

A metal for mid-tier parts: the 128K and 512K Storage Dies, the LTO-8 and LTO-9 tape upgrades and the LTO Tape Drive. Smelted from Raw Neodymium, its ore or its dust.

How to get it

Used to make

  • 128K Storage Die
  • 512K Storage Die
  • Block of Neodymium
  • LTO Tape Drive
  • LTO-8 Tape
  • LTO-9 Tape
  • Neodymium Dust
