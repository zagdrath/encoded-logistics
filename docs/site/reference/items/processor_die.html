Processor Die

encodedlogistics:processor_die · Item · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics: Materials

The third-tier chip, etched from Doped Silicon and a Gallium Ingot with the Processor Photomask. It goes into autocrafting machines, servers and the 2M Storage Die.
