Tantalum Ingot

encodedlogistics:tantalum_ingot · Item · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics: Materials

A metal for Tantalum Capacitors, the 128K and larger item drives, the Pressurized drives and the Network Bridge. Smelted from Raw Tantalum, its ore or its dust.

  • 512K Pressurized Storage Drive
  • 512K Storage Drive
  • 8K Pressurized Storage Drive
  • Block of Tantalum
  • Network Bridge
  • Tantalum Capacitor
  • Tantalum Dust
