8K Storage Die

encodedlogistics:storage_die_8k · Item · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics: Materials

The first storage chip, etched on the Lithography Press from a Silicon Wafer and Ferrite with the Storage Photomask. It goes into the 8K drives, and four of them make a 32K Storage Die.

8K Storage Die

Storage tier: 8K

Works with

  • 8K Storage Drive
  • 8K Fluid Storage Drive
  • 8K Pressurized Storage Drive
  • 8K Energy Storage Drive

How to get it
