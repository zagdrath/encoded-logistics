Storage Blade

encodedlogistics:storage_blade · Item · Stacks to 16 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics: Parts & Tools

A Blade Enclosure blade that opens two Storage Drive bays (the Drives tab) as network storage.

Storage Blade

Shift: A Blade Enclosure blade: opens six Storage Drive bays (the Drives tab) as network storage, as a NAS does.
