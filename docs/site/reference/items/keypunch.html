Card Punch

encodedlogistics:keypunch · Block · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics

Punches a crafting recipe, typed in by item name on its green screen, onto a blank Punch Card. Nothing is used up but the card. Needs a Midrange System online on its network.

Card Punch

Shift: Punches a crafting recipe, typed in by item name, onto blank Punch Cards. Needs a Midrange System online on its network.

Works with

  • Punch Card
  • Card Reader
  • Midrange System

On a network

It counts as a device of type KEYPUNCH in Work with Devices and in ELCL; the first one on a network is named KEYPUNCH01. See Device types.

How to get it

Used to make

Nothing.

As a block

Hardness2.5
Blast resistance6
Toolany pickaxe (needed for it to drop)
Light level0 in its default state

Where it's documented

  • The Midrange line (Crafting and automation)
  • Progression (Getting started)
  • Screens (Terminal OS and ELCL)
