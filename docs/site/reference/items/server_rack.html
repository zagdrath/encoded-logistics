Server Rack

encodedlogistics:server_rack · Block · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics

A 42U rack, one block wide, three tall and two deep, for rack devices. Cables (and FE from any mod) join it at its connection points on the top, bottom and back. Right-click the front or back to open its doors; sneak-right-click the front for its screen.

Server Rack

Shift: A 42U rack, 1 wide, 3 tall and 2 deep, for Firewalls, Routers and UPSes. Cables join it at the back and on top. Right-click the front or back to open its doors; sneak-right-click the front for its screen.

Works with

  • Firewall
  • UPS
  • 2U Network Controller
  • Rack Console
  • Rack Elevation

How to get it

Used to make

Nothing.

As a block

Hardness3
Blast resistance1,200
Toolany pickaxe (needed for it to drop)
Light level0 in its default state
