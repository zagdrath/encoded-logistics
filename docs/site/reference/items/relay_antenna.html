Relay Antenna

encodedlogistics:relay_antenna · Block · Stacks to 64 · Creative tab: Encoded Logistics

Wireless coverage for Handheld Terminals linked to its network: reach.relayBaseRange (default 32) blocks around it in its own dimension, plus reach.relayRangePerTransceiver (default 32) for each Optical Transceiver in its four slots. Uses one lane.

Relay Antenna
