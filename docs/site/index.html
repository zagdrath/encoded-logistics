Encoded Logistics

Storage networks and automation with server racks, autocrafting jobs and a green-screen terminal operating system. For Minecraft 26.3 on NeoForge.

Encoded Logistics builds your base's storage into a network you run like a data centre. Cables carry lanes from a Network Controller to drives, terminals, ports and server racks. Autocrafting runs as jobs on Fabricators, Gateways and Schedulers. A Terminal Desk gives you a whole green-screen operating system, the Terminal OS, with its own command language (ELCL) for programs, scheduled jobs and triggers.

This site documents version 1.4.0+26.3 of the mod.

Requirements#

NeedsVersion
Minecraft26.3
NeoForge26.3.0.0-beta or later
OptionalJEI, Jade, and Arcforge 2.5.0 or later

None of the optional mods is needed to play. JEI adds recipe lookups and search syncing, Jade adds tooltips (lanes, status), and Arcforge's machines can join a network through the Small Wireless Bridge.

The mod has no generator of its own. Its machines and networks run on FE (Forge Energy), so you'll need another mod that makes FE.

Where to start#

  1. Your first network takes you from vanilla materials to a working storage network with a terminal, step by step.
  2. Progression maps the rest of the tech path, from the Lithography Press to a full rack of servers.
  3. What a network is explains the ideas everything else rests on: controllers, cables, lanes and power.
