Encoded Logistics starts with vanilla materials and a supply of FE, and ends with racks of servers running
autocrafting jobs and scripts. These two pages get you going:

1. **{{page getting-started/first-network}}**: from sand and iron to a working storage network with a terminal. It
   covers the first silicon, the {{item lithography_press}}, the first dies, and then the six blocks and items every
   network starts with.
2. **{{page getting-started/progression}}**: the tech path after that, tier by tier, with what each new material
   unlocks and where it's documented.

## Before you begin

- **Bring your own power.** The mod has no generator. The Lithography Press and every network run on FE from another
  mod: a generator, a solar panel or anything else that pushes FE into a block. A {{item creative_capacitor_bank}}
  (creative mode only) stands in for one while you try things out.
- **Install JEI if you can.** Every recipe on these pages is also in JEI, including the Lithography Press's own
  recipe category. See {{page integrations/jei}}.
- **Hold Shift on an item.** Most of the mod's blocks and items have a short description in their tooltip.

## The ideas in one paragraph

A **network** is everything joined by cables to one {{item network_controller}} structure. The controller buffers
energy and hands out **lanes**: each device (a Drive Bay, a terminal, a port) needs a lane to work, and each cable
carries only so many. Drives in Drive Bays are the network's storage, and terminals let you browse it. When you want
the details, read {{page networks/index}} next.
