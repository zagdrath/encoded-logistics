---
Encoded Logistics needs no other mod, but it works with three optional ones. Each integration switches itself on when
the other mod is installed and does nothing otherwise; there is nothing to configure.

| Mod | What you get | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Arcforge (2.5.0 or later) | Arcforge machines on the network through the Small Wireless Bridge (read, control, power and feed them), Arcforge gases in Pressurized drives, and Arc Crusher dusts for the mod's metals | {{page integrations/arcforge}} |
| JEI | The Lithography recipe category, recipe transfer into the Fabrication Terminal and the Schematic Encoder, dragging items onto filters and ghost slots, and the terminals' search sync | {{page integrations/jei}} |
| Jade | Tooltips for the Network Controller, cables, the Power Inlet, the Segment Isolator, the Lithography Press and the Drive Bay | {{page integrations/jade}} |

Developers who want to see how an integration is put together can read {{page developers/arcforge-integration}}.
