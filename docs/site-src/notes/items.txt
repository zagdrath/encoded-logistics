# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Networks: controller, power, cables

[encodedlogistics:network_controller]
The heart of a network: it hands out the lanes and holds the network's energy buffer. Touching controller blocks join into one structure, which must be a single block, a line or a hollow frame (only the edges of its box) no bigger than {{config controller.maxSize default}} blocks on any side; each controller face with a cable or device on it adds lanes. A network has one controller structure.
works: encodedlogistics:power_inlet, encodedlogistics:capacitor_bank, encodedlogistics:network_cable, encodedlogistics:handheld_terminal
page: networks/controllers

[encodedlogistics:power_inlet]
Brings FE from another mod into a network's energy pool. Energy goes in through its front face only (it faces you when placed); cables attach on the other five faces. It uses no lanes.
works: encodedlogistics:network_controller, encodedlogistics:capacitor_bank
page: networks/power

[encodedlogistics:capacitor_bank]
Extra energy storage for a network: on a controller's network it becomes part of the energy pool, holding {{config power.capacitorCapacity default}} FE. It uses no lanes, and its faces can also take and give FE directly. Hold a Handheld Terminal against it (use and hold) to charge the terminal.
works: encodedlogistics:network_controller, encodedlogistics:power_inlet, encodedlogistics:handheld_terminal
page: networks/power

[encodedlogistics:creative_capacitor_bank]
Creative only: an endless energy source and sink. It is always full, gives any FE asked of it and takes any offered, on its network and on every face. It has no recipe and can't be broken in survival.
works: encodedlogistics:network_controller
page: networks/power

[encodedlogistics:segment_isolator]
An inline housing that splits a cable run into two separate networks (segments): lanes, storage, devices and energy don't cross it. Sneak-use a Link Card on one half to store that segment, so a rack switch, Router, Trunk Port or Optical Multiplexer can serve it.
works: encodedlogistics:link_card, encodedlogistics:trunk_port, encodedlogistics:l3_switch, encodedlogistics:router
page: networks/segments

[encodedlogistics:cable_anchor]
A small plug you put on a cable face to stop the cable connecting that way, for example to keep two parallel cables from joining. A wrench, or sneak-use with an empty hand, takes it off.
works: encodedlogistics:network_cable, encodedlogistics:dense_network_cable, encodedlogistics:fiber_cable
page: networks/cables

[encodedlogistics:cable_facade]
A panel that covers one face of a cable. Mount it on a cable, then use a full block on it to give it that block's look; sneak-use with an empty hand takes the block back off, and again removes the facade. A facade doesn't stop the cable connecting on that side.
works: encodedlogistics:network_cable, encodedlogistics:dense_network_cable, encodedlogistics:fiber_cable
page: networks/cables

[encodedlogistics:part_host]
Internal block, with no item: it holds a cable part mounted straight onto the face of a block that isn't a cable, when there's no cable in front of that face. You never place it yourself: use a part on a block face and the mod places it. The part is on the network only when the block it's mounted on is a network block.
works: encodedlogistics:access_terminal, encodedlogistics:fabrication_terminal, encodedlogistics:schematic_terminal, encodedlogistics:schematic_encoder, encodedlogistics:ingress_port, encodedlogistics:egress_port, encodedlogistics:inventory_tap, encodedlogistics:threshold_sensor, encodedlogistics:collector_plane, encodedlogistics:deployer_plane, encodedlogistics:point_to_point_link, encodedlogistics:trunk_port
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:network_cable]
The basic cable, carrying {{config lanes.perCable default}} lanes. This neutral cable connects to cables of every colour; dye it to keep runs apart.
works: encodedlogistics:cable_anchor, encodedlogistics:cable_facade
page: networks/cables

[encodedlogistics:dense_network_cable]
A thick cable carrying {{config lanes.perDenseCable default}} lanes, for trunk runs that feed many devices. This neutral cable connects to cables of every colour.
works: encodedlogistics:cable_anchor, encodedlogistics:cable_facade
page: networks/cables

[encodedlogistics:fiber_cable]
A slim cable carrying {{config lanes.perFiberCable default}} lanes, and the only cable that carries optical lines. A very long unbroken run loses lanes to span loss. This neutral cable connects to cables of every colour.
works: encodedlogistics:optical_multiplexer, encodedlogistics:optical_amplifier, encodedlogistics:optical_transport_shelf
page: networks/cables

[*_dense_network_cable]
{name}: a Dense Network Cable ({{config lanes.perDenseCable default}} lanes) that connects only to cables of its own colour and to neutral ones. Use a dye on a placed cable to recolour it, or a water bucket to wash it back to neutral.
works: encodedlogistics:dense_network_cable, encodedlogistics:cable_anchor, encodedlogistics:cable_facade
page: networks/cables

[*_network_cable]
{name}: a Network Cable ({{config lanes.perCable default}} lanes) that connects only to cables of its own colour and to neutral ones. Use a dye on a placed cable to recolour it, or a water bucket to wash it back to neutral.
works: encodedlogistics:network_cable, encodedlogistics:cable_anchor, encodedlogistics:cable_facade
page: networks/cables

[*_fiber_cable]
{name}: a Fiber Cable ({{config lanes.perFiberCable default}} lanes, with span loss on long runs) that connects only to cables of its own colour and to neutral ones. Use a dye on a placed cable to recolour it, or a water bucket to wash it back to neutral.
works: encodedlogistics:fiber_cable, encodedlogistics:cable_anchor, encodedlogistics:cable_facade
page: networks/cables

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Cable parts and port modules

[encodedlogistics:access_terminal]
A screen for the network's storage: search, sort and take or put items, fluids, gases and energy, and request crafts. Mount it on a cable face or on the face of any network block. Uses one lane.
works: encodedlogistics:drive_bay, encodedlogistics:fabrication_terminal
page: terminals/block-terminals

[encodedlogistics:fabrication_terminal]
An Access Terminal with a 3x3 crafting grid that refills itself from the network as you craft. The grid's items stay in the part and drop with it. Uses one lane.
works: encodedlogistics:access_terminal, encodedlogistics:schematic_encoder
page: terminals/block-terminals

[encodedlogistics:schematic_encoder]
A terminal that writes recipes onto blank Schematic Cards: a crafting recipe for a Fabricator, or a machine's inputs and outputs (items, fluids and gases) for a Gateway. Mounts like any cable part and uses one lane.
works: encodedlogistics:schematic_card, encodedlogistics:encoded_schematic_crafting, encodedlogistics:encoded_schematic_processing
page: crafting/schematics

[encodedlogistics:schematic_terminal]
A terminal for the network's autocrafting: every Fabricator, Gateway, Fabrication Server and Midrange System with its schematics, and every crafting job with its progress and steps, which you can cancel, hold or release. Uses one lane.
works: encodedlogistics:fabricator, encodedlogistics:gateway, encodedlogistics:fabrication_server, encodedlogistics:midrange_system, encodedlogistics:scheduler_core
page: crafting/jobs

[encodedlogistics:ingress_port]
A cable part that pulls items (or fluids, gases or energy) from the inventory it faces into the network, as its filter allows. Takes up to four port modules. Uses one lane.
works: encodedlogistics:filter_module, encodedlogistics:throughput_module, encodedlogistics:fuzzy_match_module, encodedlogistics:redstone_control_module
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:egress_port]
A cable part that pushes the items (or fluids, gases or energy) on its filter from the network into the inventory it faces; with an empty filter it moves nothing. Takes up to four port modules. Uses one lane.
works: encodedlogistics:filter_module, encodedlogistics:throughput_module, encodedlogistics:fuzzy_match_module, encodedlogistics:redstone_control_module
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:inventory_tap]
A cable part that makes the inventory (and tanks) it faces part of the network's storage where it stands, with a priority, an access mode and a filter. Uses one lane.
works: encodedlogistics:drive_bay
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:threshold_sensor]
A cable part that gives a redstone signal while the network's count of an item is above, below or equal to a number you set. Uses one lane.
works: encodedlogistics:control_interface, encodedlogistics:plc
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:point_to_point_link]
A cable part that passes items, fluids, gases, energy, redstone or lanes straight from an input endpoint to its paired outputs, without going through storage. Pick the type and direction, then pair the ends with a Link Card. Uses no lanes.
works: encodedlogistics:link_card
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:collector_plane]
A cable part that breaks the block in front of it (as an iron pickaxe would) and vacuums up dropped items into the network. A Filter or Fuzzy Match Module limits what it takes. Planes side by side join up. Uses one lane.
works: encodedlogistics:filter_module, encodedlogistics:fuzzy_match_module, encodedlogistics:deployer_plane
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:deployer_plane]
A cable part that places blocks, or drops items, from the network into the space in front of it, taking the first entry on its list that the network has. Planes side by side join up. Uses one lane.
works: encodedlogistics:collector_plane
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:trunk_port]
A cable part for VLAN trunking: it carries segments (networks beyond Segment Isolators, added with a Link Card) into the network it's on, so every Server Rack there can put its pooled devices on them. Access or Trunk mode, with a native segment.
works: encodedlogistics:segment_isolator, encodedlogistics:link_card, encodedlogistics:server_rack
page: networking/switches

[encodedlogistics:filter_module]
A port module that adds filter options to a port: deny (move everything except the entries), match by shared tag, and match exact components. It also works in a Collector Plane.
works: encodedlogistics:ingress_port, encodedlogistics:egress_port, encodedlogistics:wireless_ingress_port, encodedlogistics:wireless_egress_port, encodedlogistics:collector_plane
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:throughput_module]
Makes a port move more per operation (up to three in one port). In a Fabricator or Fabrication Server, each one (up to two) makes crafts quicker.
works: encodedlogistics:ingress_port, encodedlogistics:egress_port, encodedlogistics:wireless_ingress_port, encodedlogistics:wireless_egress_port, encodedlogistics:fabricator, encodedlogistics:fabrication_server
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:fuzzy_match_module]
Lets a port's or Collector Plane's filter entries match by tag, or by damage range: right-click a filter entry to pick how it matches.
works: encodedlogistics:ingress_port, encodedlogistics:egress_port, encodedlogistics:wireless_ingress_port, encodedlogistics:wireless_egress_port, encodedlogistics:collector_plane
page: networks/cable-parts

[encodedlogistics:redstone_control_module]
Unlocks a port's redstone modes: work only with a signal, only without one, or once per pulse. Without it a port ignores redstone.
works: encodedlogistics:ingress_port, encodedlogistics:egress_port, encodedlogistics:wireless_ingress_port, encodedlogistics:wireless_egress_port
page: networks/cable-parts

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Storage

[encodedlogistics:drive_bay]
Holds ten Storage Drives of any type and makes them the network's storage (hot storage, priority 0). Each drive's sled and fill light show on its front. Uses one lane.
works: storage_drive_*, fluid_storage_drive_*, pressurized_storage_drive_*, energy_storage_drive_*
page: storage/drive-bay

[encodedlogistics:storage_die_8k]
The first storage chip, etched on the Lithography Press from a Silicon Wafer and Ferrite with the Storage Photomask. It goes into the 8K drives, and four of them make a 32K Storage Die.
works: encodedlogistics:storage_drive_8k, encodedlogistics:fluid_storage_drive_8k, encodedlogistics:pressurized_storage_drive_8k, encodedlogistics:energy_storage_drive_8k
page: storage/drives

[encodedlogistics:storage_die_32k]
A 32K storage chip, crafted from four 8K Storage Dies. It goes into the 32K drives, and four of them make a 128K Storage Die.
works: encodedlogistics:storage_drive_32k, encodedlogistics:fluid_storage_drive_32k, encodedlogistics:pressurized_storage_drive_32k, encodedlogistics:energy_storage_drive_32k
page: storage/drives

[encodedlogistics:storage_die_128k]
A 128K storage chip, crafted from four 32K Storage Dies. It goes into the 128K drives, and four of them make a 512K Storage Die.
works: encodedlogistics:storage_drive_128k, encodedlogistics:fluid_storage_drive_128k, encodedlogistics:pressurized_storage_drive_128k, encodedlogistics:energy_storage_drive_128k
page: storage/drives

[encodedlogistics:storage_die_512k]
A 512K storage chip, crafted from four 128K Storage Dies. It goes into the 512K drives, and four of them make a 2M Storage Die.
works: encodedlogistics:storage_drive_512k, encodedlogistics:fluid_storage_drive_512k, encodedlogistics:pressurized_storage_drive_512k, encodedlogistics:energy_storage_drive_512k
page: storage/drives

[encodedlogistics:storage_die_2m]
The largest storage chip, crafted from four 512K Storage Dies. It goes into the 2M drives.
works: encodedlogistics:storage_drive_2m, encodedlogistics:fluid_storage_drive_2m, encodedlogistics:pressurized_storage_drive_2m, encodedlogistics:energy_storage_drive_2m
page: storage/drives

[storage_drive_*]
{name}: stores items for the network once it's in a drive holder. Its tier sets its bytes (8K is 8,192 bytes, each tier four times the last); a byte holds 8 items, each kind it holds reserves 1/128 of the drive, and it holds at most {{config storage.driveTypeLimit default}} kinds.
works: encodedlogistics:drive_bay, encodedlogistics:disk_drive, encodedlogistics:nas, encodedlogistics:san, encodedlogistics:storage_blade
page: storage/drives

[fluid_storage_drive_*]
{name}: stores fluids for the network once it's in a drive holder, a bucket (1,000 mB) a byte. Its tier sets its bytes (8K is 8,192 bytes, each tier four times the last); each kind it holds reserves 1/128 of the drive, up to {{config storage.driveTypeLimit default}} kinds.
works: encodedlogistics:drive_bay, encodedlogistics:disk_drive, encodedlogistics:nas, encodedlogistics:san, encodedlogistics:storage_blade
page: storage/drives

[pressurized_storage_drive_*]
{name}: stores gases and chemicals from other mods (Arcforge's gases) once it's in a drive holder, 1,000 of the gas's own unit a byte. Without a mod that provides gases there is nothing for it to hold.
works: encodedlogistics:drive_bay, encodedlogistics:disk_drive, encodedlogistics:nas, encodedlogistics:san, encodedlogistics:storage_blade
page: storage/drives

[energy_storage_drive_*]
{name}: holds 1,000 FE per byte of its tier (an 8K drive holds 8,192,000 FE). In any drive holder its FE is part of the network's energy pool, like a Capacitor Bank; it keeps its charge when taken out, and charges or powers other things as a battery item.
works: encodedlogistics:drive_bay, encodedlogistics:disk_drive, encodedlogistics:nas, encodedlogistics:san, encodedlogistics:storage_blade
page: storage/drives

[encodedlogistics:lto_tape_drive]
The read/write head for a Tape Library's drive bay. A library needs at least one to archive items to its tapes and recall them.
works: encodedlogistics:tape_library_4u, encodedlogistics:tape_library_6u, lto_tape_*
page: storage/tapes

[encodedlogistics:lto_tape_6]
Cold storage for a Tape Library: 8M bytes (8 x 1,048,576), in the same byte model as Storage Drives. It keeps what's on it when taken out, and upgrades to LTO-7 in a crafting grid.
works: encodedlogistics:tape_library_4u, encodedlogistics:tape_library_6u, encodedlogistics:lto_tape_drive
page: storage/tapes

[encodedlogistics:lto_tape_7]
Cold storage for a Tape Library: 32M bytes, and reads and writes a little faster than LTO-6. Upgrading a tape in a crafting grid keeps what's on it.
works: encodedlogistics:tape_library_4u, encodedlogistics:tape_library_6u, encodedlogistics:lto_tape_drive
page: storage/tapes

[encodedlogistics:lto_tape_8]
Cold storage for a Tape Library: 128M bytes, faster again than LTO-7. Upgrading a tape in a crafting grid keeps what's on it.
works: encodedlogistics:tape_library_4u, encodedlogistics:tape_library_6u, encodedlogistics:lto_tape_drive
page: storage/tapes

[encodedlogistics:lto_tape_9]
Cold storage for a Tape Library: 512M bytes, faster again than LTO-8. Upgrading a tape in a crafting grid keeps what's on it.
works: encodedlogistics:tape_library_4u, encodedlogistics:tape_library_6u, encodedlogistics:lto_tape_drive
page: storage/tapes

[encodedlogistics:lto_tape_10]
The largest tape: 2G bytes of cold storage for a Tape Library, and the fastest to read and write. Upgrading a tape in a crafting grid keeps what's on it.
works: encodedlogistics:tape_library_4u, encodedlogistics:tape_library_6u, encodedlogistics:lto_tape_drive
page: storage/tapes

A blank card for the Schematic Encoder to write a recipe onto. Also an ingredient of the Link Card and the schematic terminals.

[encodedlogistics:encoded_schematic_crafting]
A crafting recipe written by the Schematic Encoder: up to nine ingredients, one each, and the result. Put it in a Fabricator (or a Fabrication Server or Blade Enclosure) so the network can craft it.

A machine's inputs (up to nine) and outputs (up to three), items, fluids or gases, written by the Schematic Encoder. Put it in a Gateway next to the machine.

page: crafting/schematics

[encodedlogistics:resource_entry]
Internal: a stand-in for a fluid or gas and an amount, used in ghost slots (filters, Processing Schematics, the Gateway's stock row). You can't obtain it, and storage never holds it; it shows the fluid's or gas's name and amount.

[encodedlogistics:fabricator]

The heart of a Scheduler: it plans and runs the network's crafting jobs. Build a solid cuboid of Scheduler blocks (no side longer than {{config autocrafting.schedulerMaxSize default}}) with exactly one Core; Thread Units add threads and Job Buffers add job memory. The whole structure uses one lane.

page: crafting/schedulers

[encodedlogistics:job_buffer]
A Scheduler block that adds {{config autocrafting.jobBufferMemory default}} job memory, so the Scheduler takes bigger jobs.
works: encodedlogistics:scheduler_core, encodedlogistics:thread_unit
page: crafting/schedulers

[encodedlogistics:thread_unit]
A Scheduler block that adds a thread: another job at a time, or more crafts at once for a large job. It glows while jobs run.
works: encodedlogistics:scheduler_core, encodedlogistics:job_buffer
page: crafting/schedulers

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- The Midrange line

[encodedlogistics:expansion_cabinet]
Stands directly beside a Midrange System, facing the same way: one more crafting thread, a second diskette slot and another batch job. Anywhere else it does nothing.
works: encodedlogistics:midrange_system, encodedlogistics:diskette_8in
page: crafting/midrange
