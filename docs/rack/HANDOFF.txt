ENCODED LOGISTICS - HANDOFF: SERVER RACK + RACK DEVICES (Firewall, Router, UPS)
==============================================================================
Mod id: encodedlogistics      Target: Minecraft 26.3 / NeoForge 26.3      Terminology: lanes.
A 42U enclosed rack multiblock with animated doors, an elevation GUI, a data-driven unit HUD popup, and the first three
rack devices. Matches the attached 42U reference (proportions, mesh front door, split rear doors, two-piece side panels,
vented roof, casters) - unbranded: a blank badge plate sits where a logo would be.
Art follows docs/TEXTURE_STYLE.md (style_kit primitives; black powder-coat variant of the steel ramp). Every preview
in previews/ was rendered FROM these files (models, textures) with the door rotations applied exactly as specified.
Paths are relative to the repo root; the zip mirrors them under src/.

=====================================================================================================
1. MULTIBLOCK
=====================================================================================================
Footprint: 1 wide x 3 tall x 2 deep (6 blocks). Block id encodedlogistics:server_rack; BlockState: facing (horizontal),
part = master | dummy (dummies also store their offset if convenient, e.g. an IntegerProperty 0..5, or look up via BE).
  MASTER = the MIDDLE block of the FRONT column. Reason: model elements must stay within -16..32 px, and the whole rack
  (48 tall, 32 deep) then fits ONE static block model on the master: x 0..16, y -16..32, z 0..32 (front at z = 0).
  Placement: the player targets a block -> the rack's bottom-front block goes there, master = one above it; the other
  four blocks are: bottom-front, top-front, and the three blocks behind (toward the rack's back = +z for facing north).
  Facing = opposite the player's look (front faces the player). Fail placement if any of the 6 positions is not
  replaceable; show "Not enough room".
  Breaking ANY part breaks all six; drops one Server Rack item + every installed device (with its data components) +
  any Optical Transceivers in Routers.
  Dummies: RenderShape.INVISIBLE, full collision/selection shapes as if one 1x3x2 box (see 6 for the unit outline).
  Light: does not occlude (it's mesh); propagatesSkylightDown = false is fine.
Block entity: RackBlockEntity on the master only: devices (U -> RackDevice instance), door state (front/rear open flag +
  animation progress for the client), network node.
Static model: blockstates/server_rack.json -> part=master uses models/block/rack/rack_frame.json (y-rotated by facing),
  part=dummy uses rack_empty.json. The frame model includes posts, two-piece side panels with a 0.5 px seam and lock
  points, vented roof with cable-entry slots, plinth, casters + levelling feet (the rack sits 1 px off the ground),
  and the front + rear 19" rails (zinc, square holes, U numbers 1..42 from the bottom).
Vertical layout (master local y): -16..-15 casters | -15..-13 plinth | -13..29 the 42U space (U1 = y -13..-12, Uk = y
  -14+k .. -13+k, 1 px per U) | 29..32 roof.  rack_models.U_y(u) = -13 + (u-1).

=====================================================================================================
2. RENDERER (RackBlockEntityRenderer on the master)
=====================================================================================================
Register as additional models: rack_door_front, rack_door_rear_left, rack_door_rear_right and every device model
(models/block/rack_device/<id>.json, <id>_on.json, <id>_fault.json). Renders, in the master's space:
  - poseStack: rotate by facing about the rack's footprint centre (x 8, z 16 in model px), then draw.
  - Doors: translate(pivot), mulPose(Axis.YP.rotationDegrees(angle * eased(progress))), translate(-pivot), draw the door
    model. Pivots/angles in door_animation.json (front -110 at (15.5,0,0.5); rear left +110 at (15.5,0,31.5); rear
    right -110 at (0.5,0,31.5)).
  - Devices: for each installed device, translate(0, U_y(u)/16, 0) and draw its model for its state (off -> <id>,
    on -> <id>_on, fault -> <id>_fault; the glow elements are full-bright via neoforge_data). Optional per-device extras
    (2.1). Cutout render type for the frame/doors (mesh holes are alpha 0) and devices.
  - Culling: skip devices when both doors are closed AND the player is farther than 24 blocks [config].
2.1 Device render extras (drawn by the device's renderer hook after its model):
  - Router: for each installed Optical Transceiver (slot i = 0..2), a 6x2-texel quad from its texture sprite (0,64)-(6,66)
    over cage i (front texture x 67+8i, y 3) - the cages visibly hold transceivers.
  - UPS: live battery % on the LCD - 7-segment digit sprites (0,64)+5*d (4x7 texels each) and '%' at (50,64), drawn
    full-bright over the LCD (x 17.., y 5 in the front texture); the 10 load LEDs (44+3i, 6) lit by load fraction
    (green 0-5, amber 6-7, red 8-9). The baked _on texture shows 6 lit as the default.
Texel densities (why the textures are the sizes they are): frame 1 texel/px; door mesh 2 texels/px; rails 4 texels/px
(so each U carries a mounting hole + a number); devices 8 texels/px on 128x128 sheets (a 1U front is 104x8 texels).
UVs in the JSONs are already computed for those sizes.

=====================================================================================================
3. DOORS
=====================================================================================================
  Right-click the FRONT face: toggle the front door. Right-click the REAR face: toggle both rear leaves.
  Shift-right-click the front: open the GUI (works with the door open or closed).
  Animation: 10 ticks, smoothstep easing both ways, client-side interpolation; the server just stores open/closed and
  syncs it (block event or BE update). Sounds: block.rack.door_open on open, block.rack.door_close on close
  (sounds.json; 2 synthesised variants each in sounds/block/rack/*.ogg - sheet-metal latch click + swing).
  Interaction while open: the unit HUD (6) and installing by hand (OPEN 4) only through an open door.
  Door models are separate parts: front leaf (x 0.5..15.5, z 0..1, mesh both sides, recessed handle + lock on the
  right at x 1.75); rear leaves (x 8..15.5 and 0.5..8, z 31..32, a handle each by the centre seam).
  Collision does not change when doors open (keep the full box).

=====================================================================================================
4. NETWORK
=====================================================================================================
  The rack connects through a cable on its REAR face (either rear block) or its TOP face (top-front or top-rear);
  cables report 'block' there, 'none' elsewhere. Each installed device is a network device using 1 lane; the rack
  itself uses 0 (OPEN 1 on power). Devices go online when the network is online and they have their lane; a device
  without a lane shows offline.

=====================================================================================================
5. U-SLOT RULES + GUI
=====================================================================================================
  Devices are 1U or 2U (RackDeviceType.size). A device at U u occupies u..u+size-1; all must be free and <= 42.
  Install: in the GUI, pick up a device item and click an empty U (the drop target spans the device's height: amber
  dashed = fits, red = blocked, tooltip "Needs 2U free in a row"); or shift-click a device item from the inventory to
  put it in the lowest free fit. Remove: shift-click a placed device (returns the item with its data).
  GUI = one screen, two halves: top 176x168, bottom 176x100 (gui/rack/inventory.png, player inventory at (8, top+17)).
  Elevation (gui/rack/elevation.png, screens/rack/elevation.json): inset (8,18,140,146), 16 rows of 9 px visible,
  scroll for 42 (U42 at the top, U1 at the bottom - rack elevation convention). Per row: U number right-aligned at
  x 23 (TEXT_MUTED; occupied rows TEXT); slot strip at x 26 (sprite rack/slot_empty 104x8). Installed devices draw
  their REAL front texture region (0,0)-(104,8n) at 1:1 (plus glow by state) spanning n rows; select outline
  rack/select_1u / select_2u (mint) at (25, row-1). Scrollbar (150,18,10,146), thumb rack/scroll_thumb. Title + "NU free".
  Selecting a placed device opens its settings panel in the top half (back button rack/back at (151,2)).

=====================================================================================================
6. RACK UNIT HUD POPUP
=====================================================================================================
Targeting (client, every frame while the crosshair is on a rack block):
  1. Take the BlockHitResult; convert the hit location into the master's local px space (undo facing).
  2. Front face (z within 0..2 px of the front, or the hit is on the front door plane) -> use it; rear face (z >= 29.5)
     -> only when the rear doors are open; otherwise no popup.
  3. y_px -> U: u = floor(y_px - (-13)) + 1, valid 1..42 (top/bottom frame -> no popup).
  4. If u is inside a device, target = that device (all of its U); else target = the empty U.
Outline: replace the vanilla block outline (RenderHighlightEvent.Block, cancel for rack blocks) with a thin box around
  the targeted DEVICE ONLY: x 1.5..14.5, z 1.75..29.25 px, y = its full U span, inflated 0.15 px; colour #5CF0B8 at
  0.85 alpha, line width 2 (screens/rack/hud.json). Empty U: no outline (or a 1 px-tall box at the U - optional).
Popup (screens/rack/hud.json; sprites gui/sprites/hud/*): a compact panel near the crosshair (offset +12,-8 GUI px,
  clamped to the screen), 9-slice hud/panel (3 px borders), min 96 / max 180 px wide, 0.92 alpha.
  - Empty U: one line "U14 - Empty" (lang gui.encodedlogistics.rack.hud.empty, an em dash).
  - Device: header = item icon (16x16) + device name + U position ("U12" or "U12-U13", en dash); divider; status line
    with a 5x5 dot (hud/dot_online | dot_offline | dot_fault) and coloured text (ACCENT / TEXT_MUTED / ERROR); then the
    device's info lines: label left (TEXT_MUTED), value right (TEXT), 10 px each; an optional bar under a line
    (hud/bar_track 64x5 + bar_fill | bar_fill_warn | bar_fill_low, stretched to the line width).
  Mockups: previews/hud_empty.png, hud_firewall.png, hud_router.png, hud_ups.png (in-world, crosshair at centre).
DATA-DRIVEN POPUP API (the popup never changes for new devices):
  interface RackDeviceInfo { Component name(); Status status();            // ONLINE / OFFLINE / FAULT
                             Component statusText();                        // e.g. "Online - on mains"
                             List<InfoLine> lines(); }
  record InfoLine(Component label, Component value, @Nullable Bar bar) {}
  record Bar(float fraction, BarStyle style) {}                             // NORMAL / WARN / LOW -> fill sprite
  Each device type implements RackDevice#describe(DescribeContext) -> RackDeviceInfo. The server sends a small
  RackUnitInfoPayload for the targeted unit when the crosshair settles on it (and every 10 ticks while it stays),
  so lines can show server-side numbers without syncing every device's full state.
  (Jade/WTHIT: OPEN 6.)

=====================================================================================================
7. ADDING A DEVICE (the whole extension surface)
=====================================================================================================
  1. Item: an item with the RackDevice data component holding its type id (+ its own state component).
  2. RackDeviceType registry entry: id, size (1 or 2), model ids (off / on / fault), the device's GUI panel factory,
     optional renderer hook (2.1), and a factory for its behaviour.
  3. Behaviour: class extends RackDevice { tick(), onInstalled(rack), onRemoved(), status(), describe() (6), save/load,
     network node (lane cost, default 1), optional energy capability }.
  4. Assets: models/block/rack_device/<id>(_on|_fault).json from tools/rack_models.device_model(name, n, glow),
     a 128x128 texture laid out like the others (front (0,0)-(104,8n), rear (0,32)-(104,32+8n), chassis texels
     (112,0)-(128,16), extras from (0,64)), its _on and _fault (+ blink .mcmeta) overlays, a 16x16 item icon, and
     popup lines. No change to the rack, the GUI elevation, the HUD or the outline.

=====================================================================================================
8. DEVICES
=====================================================================================================
8.1 FIREWALL (firewall, 1U, 1 lane) - network access control.
  Per player: view, insert, extract, craft, build (place/remove network blocks and parts) - each ON / OFF / INHERIT
  (inherit = the default policy). Default policy: No access / View only / Full access. Players with build permission
  can edit the Firewall (owner always can; owner = whoever installed it). Enforced wherever the network is used
  (terminals, ports' GUIs, crafting requests, wrench/break on network blocks). Offline Firewall -> policy still
  enforced (fail closed) [config].
  Front: ears, red accent stripe, 8 copper ports with link LEDs, status LED. Rear: IEC inlet, vents, 2 fans.
  Panel (gui/rack/firewall.png, screens/rack/firewall.json): default policy button (cycles), player list (7 visible
  rows: head, name, 5 toggles under the permission icons rack/firewall/perm_*), add-player field + Add (online
  players autocomplete), remove via right-click on a row.
  HUD lines: Default policy; Custom players (count with any non-inherit permission).
8.2 ROUTER (router, 1U, 1 lane) - routes items between network segments split by Segment Isolators.
  It must be reachable from each segment it routes: a segment is "known" when a cable of that segment connects to the
  rack the Router is in OR to a Point-to-Point lanes link / Network Bridge that ends at it (proposed: the rack's own
  network + any segment linked to the Router with a Link Card - sneak-use a Link Card on an Isolator side to record
  that segment, then on the Router). Rules: source segment -> destination segment + item filter (ghost item or tag
  via the Fuzzy rules). Each tick budget moves matching items from source storage to destination storage.
  Front: ears, blue accent, 6 ports, 3 SFP cages (transceivers drawn into them, 2.1), status LED. Rear: power + fans.
  Panel (gui/rack/router.png): segments list (dot + name, rename on click), routes list (source -> dest, filter
  ghost), "+ Add route", 3 transceiver slots (accept optical_transceiver), throughput readout + bar.
  HUD lines: Active routes; Throughput (items/s).
8.3 UPS (ups, 2U, 1 lane) - backup power.
  Stores FE. When the network's supply from Power Inlets drops below demand, the UPS supplies the difference
  (switchover within the same tick), and recharges from surplus when supply returns. Modes: Online (always conditions
  the supply - covers instantly, recharges continuously) / Standby (only engages when supply fails; lower idle drain).
  Front: ears, LCD with live battery % (2.1), 10-LED load bar, power button, vented battery bezel. Rear: inlet, 4 output
  sockets, fan. Fault = battery missing/failed or overload (> rated output).
  Panel (gui/rack/ups.png): Arcforge energy bar (controller/energy_bar at (9,23)), readouts Charge / Load / Runtime /
  Source, load bar (gold), mode button (rack/ups/mode_online | mode_standby), event log (last 3 switchovers).
  HUD lines: Battery % (with bar: normal >50%, warn 20-50%, low <20%); Load (FE/t); Runtime; Mode.

11. OPEN QUESTIONS (proposals included)
=====================================================================================================
1. Rack power: proposed the rack draws nothing itself; each device drains from the network (Firewall 2, Router 4 +1 per
   transceiver, UPS 1 standby / 2 online FE/t) [config].
2. UPS: proposed 1,000,000 FE capacity, 8,192 FE/t max output, switchover in the same tick; multiple UPSes stack -
   capacity and output add, they discharge together and recharge in parallel.
3. Router throughput: proposed 16 items/s base, +16 per transceiver (3 slots, max 64 items/s), shared across routes
   round-robin.
4. Installing devices: proposed GUI install works with the door closed (the GUI is the "admin console"); hand-install
   by using a device item on an open rack front at the targeted U [optional].
5. Firewall scope: proposed one Firewall per network is enough (a second one shows Fault "Conflicting firewall"); it is
   the mod's access-control block - no separate access block needed.
6. Jade/WTHIT: proposed the rack opts out (blacklist the rack blocks in both) so the unit HUD is the single source;
   the HUD also respects F1 and hides while a screen is open.

[ ] Front door and rear doors swing 110 deg in 10 ticks with smoothstep easing, latch/swing sounds; equipment is visible
    through the mesh when closed.
[ ] Shift-right-click opens the elevation GUI; 1U/2U install needs consecutive free U; devices show their real front.
[ ] HUD popup shows "U14 - Empty" or the device header/status/lines; the outline wraps only the targeted device.
[ ] Firewall enforces per-player permissions; Router moves items between Isolator segments by rule; UPS covers supply
    drops and recharges; LCD %, load LEDs and transceiver cages render live.
