TEXTURE RULES (update the first zip's HANDOFF section to these)
  - RACK EXTERIOR (posts/frame, side panels, roof + roof band, plinth, interior): drawn the way vanilla draws dark metal
    (netherite block, anvil, hopper, iron door): vanilla density (1 texel per model px), an 8-tone black powder-coat
    palette, brushed runs of 3-7 px one tone up/down (horizontal on plates, vertical on posts), light that steps in
    bands (top rows +1, bottom rows -1), crisp bevelled inset panels. No blended gradients, no single-pixel speckle.
  - MESH DOORS (4 texels/px): staggered hex-row openings (alpha 0 cutout), smoothly shaded frame, blank badge plate.
  - RAILS (8 texels/px): zinc shaded as a rounded strip, a 3x3 mounting hole per U, small U numbers every 5U that fit
    inside one U (counted from the bottom).
  - DEVICES (8 texels/px): faceplates, ears, accent stripes, port recesses and the UPS LCD smoothly shaded; no flat
    fills, no speckle. Texture layout (front/rear/chassis/extras regions) unchanged.
